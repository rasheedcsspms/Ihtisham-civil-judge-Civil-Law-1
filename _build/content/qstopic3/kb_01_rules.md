
# Chapter 1 · Pleadings — meaning, object and basic rules (Order VI)

## 1.1 Meaning

"**Pleading** shall mean **plaint** or **written statement**" (O. VI r. 1). A **plaint** is the statement of the **plaintiff's claim**; a **written statement** is the **defendant's defence**. Replications or further pleadings are allowed only **with leave** (O. VIII r. 9). Applications, petitions and affidavits are **not** pleadings, though the same discipline of drafting applies to them.

## 1.2 Object of pleadings

1. To bring the parties to **definite issues**, so that the **real controversy** is identified (O. XIV r. 1 — issues arise when a material proposition is **affirmed by one** party and **denied by the other**).
2. To give each side **fair notice** of the case it must meet, preventing **surprise** at the trial.
3. To **narrow** the dispute and save time and costs — facts admitted need not be proved (O. VIII r. 5; QSO Art. 113).
4. To **fix the boundaries** of evidence and relief — no evidence can be led, and no relief granted, on a case **not pleaded** ("*secundum allegata et probata*" — according to what is alleged and proved).
5. To create a **permanent record** for res judicata and appeal.

:::maxim Secundum allegata et probata
A court decides a case according to what is **pleaded and proved**. A party cannot be allowed to set up at the trial a case **inconsistent** with his pleading, and evidence led on a point not pleaded is ordinarily **ignored** — unless both parties knowingly went to trial on it and no prejudice is caused.
:::

## 1.3 The fundamental rule — O. VI r. 2

> "Every pleading shall contain, and contain only, a statement **in a concise form** of the **material facts** on which the party pleading relies for his claim or defence, as the case may be, but **not the evidence** by which they are to be proved, and shall, when necessary, be divided into **paragraphs, numbered consecutively**. **Dates, sums and numbers** shall be expressed in **figures**."

The four commandments:

1. **Plead facts, not law** — the legal conclusion (e.g., "the defendant is liable") is not a fact; but a **mixed question** or a **plea of law** (limitation, jurisdiction, res judicata) may be raised, and the **provision of law** relied on may be cited.
2. **Plead material facts only** — those facts which, if proved, **establish the cause of action** or the defence; the omission of one is fatal (the plaint may not disclose a cause of action — O. VII r. 11(a)).
3. **Plead facts, not evidence** — "the defendant borrowed Rs. 500,000 on 01.03.2024" (material fact); not "as witnessed by X and Y and as shown by his letter dated …" (evidence).
4. **Be concise** — short numbered paragraphs, one fact or allegation per paragraph, no repetition, no argument, no rhetoric.

{w:22,39,39}
|Point|Material facts|Particulars|
|Meaning|The **essential** facts constituting the cause of action or defence|The **details** of those facts — dates, items, names, amounts — that give notice of the case|
|Effect of omission|The pleading is **bad** — no cause of action / no defence|The pleading is **vague**; the court may order **further and better particulars** (O. VI r. 5)|
|Example|"The defendant obtained the gift deed by **fraud**"|"He represented on 05.01.2024 that the document was a power of attorney for the electricity connection, and obtained the plaintiff's thumb impression …"|

## 1.5 Amendment of pleadings — principles (O. VI r. 17)

1. **Liberal** — amendments necessary to determine the **real controversy** should be allowed, at any stage, even in appeal, **on costs** if necessary.
2. **Refused** where it would (i) **change the nature** of the suit or introduce a **totally new cause of action**; (ii) **take away a right accrued** to the other side by **limitation**; (iii) **withdraw an admission** to the prejudice of the other side; (iv) be **mala fide** or cause **injustice** not compensable by costs.
3. **Clerical or arithmetical** mistakes may be corrected under s. 152 CPC.

# Chapter 2 · Particulars of a plaint (Order VII)

## 2.1 The plaint — what it must contain (O. VII r. 1)

![Anatomy of a plaint](plaint_anatomy.png)

{w:8,30,62}
|(a)|**Name of the court**|"In the Court of the Civil Judge (1st Class), Islamabad (West)" — the court of the **lowest grade** competent to try it (s. 15 CPC)|
|(b)|**Plaintiff's** name, description and residence|Name, parentage ("son / daughter / wife of"), caste if customary, age, occupation, **full address**, **CNIC** in practice|
|(c)|**Defendant's** name, description and residence|"So far as they can be ascertained"; for the **Government**, the **Federation of Pakistan through the Secretary**, Ministry … (s. 79 CPC); a company through its chief executive; a firm in the firm name (O. XXX)|
|(d)|Statement that a party is a **minor or of unsound mind**|"A.B., minor, through his father and next friend C.D." (O. XXXII)|
|(e)|**Facts constituting the cause of action** and **when it arose**|The heart of the plaint — every material fact, in chronological order; the **date** on which the right to sue accrued (and any later dates of denial or threat)|
|(f)|**Facts showing that the court has jurisdiction**|**Territorial** (ss. 16–20 CPC — where the property is, or the defendant resides, or the cause of action arose); **pecuniary** (value within the court's limits); **subject-matter** (no bar — s. 9 CPC)|
|(g)|The **relief** claimed|Stated **specifically**, simply or in the **alternative** (r. 7); general relief need not be prayed|
|(h)|**Set-off allowed or portion relinquished**|The amount, if any|
|(i)|**Value** of the subject-matter for **jurisdiction** and for **court fee**|"The value of the suit for the purposes of jurisdiction is Rs. … and for court fee Rs. …; court fee of Rs. … has been affixed"|

## 2.2 Further particulars — O. VII rr. 2 to 8

1. **r. 2 — money suits:** state the **precise amount** claimed; for **mesne profits** or **unsettled accounts**, the **approximate** amount.
2. **r. 3 — immovable property:** a description **sufficient to identify** it — **boundaries**, **khasra / khewat / khatooni numbers**, **plot, street, sector** (e.g., "House No. 12, Street 4, Sector G-9/2, Islamabad, bounded: North — …").
3. **r. 4 — representative suits:** show the plaintiff's **actual existing interest** and the **steps** taken to entitle him to sue (e.g., permission under O. I r. 8; s. 91–92 CPC consent).
4. **r. 5** — show that the **defendant is interested** in the subject-matter and **liable** to answer the claim.
5. **r. 6 — limitation:** if the suit is **beyond time**, show the **ground of exemption** (e.g., ss. 6–8 disability, s. 18 fraud, s. 19 acknowledgment, s. 20 part-payment of the Limitation Act).
6. **r. 7** — the **relief** to be stated **specifically**, simply or in the alternative.
7. **r. 8** — reliefs on **separate grounds** to be stated **separately and distinctly**.

## 2.3 Papers filed with the plaint

1. **List of documents** endorsed on or annexed to the plaint (O. VII r. 9(1)); the **documents sued upon** (the agreement, pronote, cheque) **produced** with the plaint and a **copy** filed (r. 14(1)); a **list of other documents** relied upon (r. 14(2)); where a document is not in his possession, **in whose possession** it is (r. 15); shop-book entries with a certified copy (r. 17). A document **not produced or listed** cannot be received in evidence **without leave** (r. 18).
2. **Copies of the plaint** for each defendant **plus two**, and **draft summons forms** with **process fee** (r. 9(1A)).
3. **Address for service** within the district (rr. 19–20) — failure may lead to **dismissal** after an opportunity (r. 21).
4. **List of legal representatives** in suits that survive (r. 26).
5. **Vakalatnama** (power of the advocate), **affidavit** in support (practice), and **court fee** stamps.
6. **List of witnesses** — under O. XVI r. 1 within the time fixed by the court (and, in a **Family Court**, **with the plaint** — Family Courts Act s. 7(2)).
7. **Notice under s. 80 CPC** — a suit against the **Government or a public officer** for an official act needs **two months' notice** delivered before suit (or leave to sue without notice in urgent cases — s. 80(2) where applicable); the plaint must state that it was **served**.
8. **Notice under special laws** where required (e.g., Cantonment, CDA, Pakistan Railways laws).

## 2.4 Valuation and court fee — the essentials

1. **Two values** — for **jurisdiction** (Suits Valuation Act 1887; s. 15 CPC) and for **court fee** (Court Fees Act 1870); for most suits they are the **same** (Suits Valuation Act s. 8).
2. **Ad valorem** fee (Court Fees Act s. 7 and Schedule I) — on the **amount claimed** in money suits; on the **market value** in suits for possession of land or houses (subject to the valuation rules); on the **value stated** by the plaintiff in suits for **declaration with consequential relief**, **injunction**, **accounts** (s. 7(iv)) — the plaintiff must state the amount at which he values the relief.
3. **Fixed** fee (Schedule II) — e.g., a suit for a **declaratory decree where no consequential relief** is prayed (Schedule II, Art. 17(iii)); certain petitions and applications.
4. **Specific performance** — ad valorem on the **amount of consideration** (s. 7(x)).
5. **Insufficient valuation or stamp** — the court must give **time** to correct; only then is the plaint **rejected** (O. VII r. 11(b), (c)); deficiency may be made good later (s. 149 CPC).

## 2.5 Return and rejection — reminders

1. **Return (O. VII r. 10)** — for presentation to the **proper court** (want of jurisdiction), with the dates and reasons endorsed.
2. **Rejection (O. VII r. 11)** — (a) **no cause of action** disclosed; (b) **undervaluation** not corrected; (c) **insufficient stamp** not supplied; (d) suit **barred by any law** on the face of the plaint. Decided on the **plaint alone** (and documents annexed); rejection does not bar a **fresh plaint** (r. 13); it is a **decree** (s. 2(2)) and appealable. (Detailed in Civil Law-I, Topic 1.)

# Chapter 3 · Particulars of a written statement (Order VIII)

![Structure of a written statement](ws_structure.png)

## 3.1 Filing — O. VIII r. 1, r. 10

The defendant **may**, and if required by the court **shall**, present a written statement **at or before the first hearing** or within the time permitted — **not ordinarily exceeding thirty days** (proviso). If a party required to file one fails, the court **may pronounce judgment** against him or make such order as it thinks fit (r. 10) — a discretion to be exercised judicially, often after **closing the right of defence** and recording evidence.

## 3.2 What a written statement must do — rr. 2 to 5

1. **r. 2 — new facts must be specially pleaded:** all matters showing the suit is **not maintainable**, that the transaction is **void or voidable**, and all grounds which would **take the plaintiff by surprise** or raise issues not arising from the plaint — e.g., **fraud, limitation, release, payment, performance, illegality** (and res judicata, estoppel, waiver, bar of s. 10 or O. II r. 2, want of notice, non-joinder).
2. **r. 3 — denial to be specific:** a **general denial** is insufficient; each allegation not admitted must be dealt with **specifically**, except **damages**.
3. **r. 4 — no evasive denial:** answer the **point of substance** — "the defendant denies that he received Rs. 500,000 **or any part thereof**", or state how much he received.
4. **r. 5 — effect of not denying:** every allegation **not denied** specifically or by necessary implication, or **stated to be not admitted**, is **taken to be admitted** (except against a person under disability); the court may still require proof.

## 3.3 Three ways to answer an allegation

{w:22,78}
|**Admit**|"Para 1 is **admitted**." / "Para 2 is admitted to the extent that …; the rest is denied."|
|**Deny**|"Para 3 is **incorrect and denied**. The defendant never borrowed any amount from the plaintiff." — always add the defendant's **own version** in a sentence|
|**Not admit**|"Para 4 is **not admitted** for want of knowledge; the plaintiff be put to strict proof." — for facts outside the defendant's knowledge|

**Avoid:** "Para 5 is incorrect as stated" without saying what **is** correct (an evasive denial); "the plaintiff be put to strict proof" for a fact within the defendant's own knowledge (it is a non-denial — treated as admitted).

## 3.4 Preliminary (legal) objections — the usual list

1. The plaint **does not disclose a cause of action** (O. VII r. 11(a)).
2. The plaintiff has **no locus standi**.
3. The suit is **barred by limitation** (Limitation Act, Article …).
4. The court lacks **territorial / pecuniary / subject-matter jurisdiction** (s. 9 CPC; special-law bar).
5. **Res judicata** (s. 11) / **res sub judice** (s. 10) / **O. II r. 2** bar.
6. **Non-joinder or misjoinder** of necessary parties (O. I rr. 9, 13).
7. **Undervaluation**; **insufficient court fee**.
8. **Want of notice** under s. 80 CPC (against the Government).
9. Suit for a **bare declaration** is barred by the **proviso to s. 42 SRA** (further relief available but not claimed).
10. The plaintiff is **estopped** by his words and conduct (QSO Art. 114); has **not come with clean hands**; is guilty of **laches**.
11. The suit is **bad for multifariousness**; is **false, frivolous and vexatious** (s. 35A CPC — special costs).

Only plead objections that **arise on the facts** — a shotgun list loses credibility.

## 3.5 Set-off and counter-claim

1. **Legal set-off (r. 6)** — in a **money suit**, an **ascertained** sum **legally recoverable** from the plaintiff, within the court's pecuniary limits, with both parties in the **same character**, pleaded at the **first hearing**; the written statement then has the effect of a **plaint in a cross-suit**; **court fee** is payable on it.
2. **Equitable set-off** — unascertained cross-claims arising from the **same transaction**, in the court's discretion.
3. **Counter-claim** — the Islamabad CPC has **no** O. VIII r. 6A; an independent claim is pursued by a **separate suit** (consolidated) or as a cross-claim **with court fee** where the court allows (Civil Law-I, Topic 1).
4. **rr. 7–9** — separate grounds of defence stated separately; **new grounds** arising after the suit may be raised; **subsequent pleadings** only with leave.
5. **rr. 11–13** — **address for service** (failure may lead to the defence being **struck out**) and the **list of legal representatives**.

# Chapter 4 · Drafting technique

## 4.1 The standard structure of every pleading

1. **Heading** — court, suit number ("Civil Suit No. …… of 2026"), **title** (parties with full descriptions), and the **nature of the suit** with the provision ("Suit for declaration and permanent injunction").
2. **Opening line** — "The plaintiff respectfully submits as under:—" / "Written statement on behalf of defendant No. 1:—".
3. **Body** — short numbered paragraphs in **chronological** order: the parties and their relationship → the transaction → the plaintiff's performance → the defendant's breach / wrong → the demand and refusal / notice → the **cause of action** paragraph → **jurisdiction** → **limitation** (if relevant) → **valuation and court fee** → (other formal paragraphs: no other suit pending; s. 80 notice served).
4. **Prayer** — "It is, therefore, respectfully prayed that a decree may kindly be passed in favour of the plaintiff and against the defendant(s) for: (a) …; (b) …; (c) costs of the suit; (d) any other relief which this Honourable Court deems fit and proper."
5. **Signatures** — "Plaintiff" and "Through counsel (name, advocate)", with the **date and place**.
6. **Verification** — "Verified on oath at Islamabad on this …… day of …… 2026 that the contents of paras 1 to …… are true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief and those of paras …… to …… are based on information received and believed to be true, and nothing has been concealed therefrom." — signed by the deponent.
7. **Annexures** — list of documents, list of witnesses, affidavit, address for service, vakalatnama.

## 4.2 Language and style

1. Use the **third person** ("the plaintiff", "the defendant") — never "I" or "you".
2. **Past tense** for completed events; **present** for continuing states.
3. Write **dates and sums in figures** — "on 15.03.2025", "Rs. 1,500,000/-".
4. Describe property **exactly** (O. VII r. 3) and refer to documents by **date and description**, annexing copies as "**Annex-A**".
5. **One idea per paragraph**; no case law, no arguments, no adjectives of abuse.
6. **Plead in the alternative** where facts justify (e.g., specific performance, **or in the alternative** refund of earnest money with damages).
7. **Consistency** — the cause of action, the reliefs and the valuation must match.

## 4.3 Common mistakes

1. Wrong court (pecuniary or territorial) or wrong title (Government not sued through the Secretary; minor not through a next friend).
2. No **date of the cause of action**; vague "the defendant is threatening".
3. Pleading **evidence** or **law reports**; reproducing whole documents.
4. **Bare declaration** when **possession or injunction** is available (s. 42 proviso SRA).
5. No **readiness and willingness** in a specific-performance plaint (s. 24(b) SRA; Form 47).
6. Missing **boundaries / khasra numbers** for land.
7. **Valuation** not stated, or inconsistent with the relief; court fee not affixed.
8. No **verification**, or verification of every paragraph "on knowledge" though some are on information.
9. In a written statement: **general denial**; "incorrect as stated"; failing to plead **limitation, fraud, payment** (O. VIII r. 2) — they cannot be raised later without amendment.
