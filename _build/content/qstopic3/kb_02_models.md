
# Chapter 5 · Model plaints

All names, numbers and amounts below are **illustrative**. In the examination, adapt the facts given; keep the **structure**, the **formal paragraphs** and the **prayer**.

## 5.1 Suit for recovery of money (loan) — Appendix A, Form No. 1

:::eg Plaint
**IN THE COURT OF THE CIVIL JUDGE 1st CLASS, ISLAMABAD (WEST)**

Civil Suit No. …… of 2026

Ahmad Ali son of Muhammad Ali, aged about 45 years, businessman, resident of House No. 12, Street 4, Sector G-9/2, Islamabad (CNIC No. …) — **Plaintiff**

**Versus**

Bilal Khan son of Sher Khan, aged about 40 years, resident of House No. 7, Street 10, Sector I-8/3, Islamabad — **Defendant**

**SUIT FOR RECOVERY OF RS. 1,500,000/-**

The plaintiff respectfully submits as under:—

1. That the parties are known to each other and have business relations.

2. That on 10.01.2024 the defendant requested the plaintiff for a loan of Rs. 1,500,000/- for his business, repayable on or before 10.07.2024. The plaintiff advanced the said amount through cheque No. … dated 10.01.2024 drawn on … Bank, which was encashed by the defendant. The defendant executed a receipt-cum-agreement of the same date, attested by two witnesses (Annex-A).

3. That on the due date and thereafter the plaintiff repeatedly demanded repayment, but the defendant kept on putting him off on one pretext or another.

4. That on 15.09.2025 the plaintiff served a legal notice on the defendant (Annex-B), which he received but did not reply to or comply with.

5. That the defendant has not paid any part of the loan and is liable to pay Rs. 1,500,000/- to the plaintiff.

6. That the **cause of action** arose on 10.01.2024 when the loan was advanced, on 10.07.2024 when it became payable and was not paid, and on 15.09.2025 and thereafter when the defendant failed to pay despite the notice; it is continuing.

7. That the defendant resides and the cause of action arose at Islamabad within the territorial jurisdiction of this Court, and the value of the suit is within its pecuniary jurisdiction.

8. That the suit is within **time** (Limitation Act, Article 57 — three years from the date of the loan).

9. That the value of the suit for the purposes of **jurisdiction and court fee** is Rs. 1,500,000/-, on which the requisite court fee has been affixed.

It is, therefore, respectfully prayed that a **decree for Rs. 1,500,000/-** may kindly be passed in favour of the plaintiff and against the defendant, with **costs** of the suit, and with such **profit / compensation** for the period of default as the Court deems just, and any other relief which this Honourable Court deems fit and proper.

Plaintiff — Through: (name), Advocate High Court, Islamabad. Dated: …….2026

**VERIFICATION:** Verified on oath at Islamabad on this …… day of …… 2026 that the contents of paras 1 to 9 are true and correct to my knowledge and belief and nothing has been concealed. — Plaintiff
:::

**Notes:** (1) a written loan agreement is a document of a **financial obligation** — it must be **attested by two men or one man and two women** and proved by **two attesting witnesses** (QSO Arts. 17, 79); (2) claim **compensation** for the period of default (Contract Act s. 73) rather than "**interest**", which is contentious after the Federal Shariat Court's 2022 *riba* judgment; (3) a suit on a **negotiable instrument** (cheque, pronote) may be brought under the **summary procedure of O. XXXVII** — then the plaint states that it is filed under O. XXXVII and the summons is in Form 4 of Appendix B.

## 5.2 Suit for declaration and permanent injunction (SRA ss. 42, 54)

:::eg Plaint (body)
**SUIT FOR DECLARATION AND PERMANENT INJUNCTION**

1. That the plaintiff is the **owner in possession** of the house constructed on Plot No. 45, measuring 1 kanal, Street 6, Sector F-11/3, Islamabad, bounded: North — Plot No. 44; South — Plot No. 46; East — Street 6; West — Plot No. 52 (the "suit property"), having purchased it from its allottee vide registered sale deed No. … dated 05.05.2015 (Annex-A), and the transfer stands recorded in his name in the record of the Capital Development Authority (defendant No. 2) (Annex-B).

2. That the plaintiff has been in peaceful possession of the suit property since 05.05.2015 and has been paying the property tax and utility bills (Annex-C).

3. That on 01.02.2026 the plaintiff learnt that defendant No. 1, a stranger, has applied to defendant No. 2 for transfer of the suit property in his name on the basis of a forged general power of attorney purportedly executed by the plaintiff on 10.12.2025 (Annex-D).

4. That the plaintiff never executed any power of attorney in favour of defendant No. 1 or anyone else; his alleged signatures and thumb impression on it are **forged**. (Particulars of fraud: the plaintiff was abroad from 01.12.2025 to 20.01.2026, as shown by the entries in his passport (Annex-E); the stamp paper was purchased in the name of a non-existent vendor; the alleged attesting witnesses are unknown to the plaintiff.)

5. That on 05.02.2026 defendant No. 1 came to the suit property with armed persons and **threatened** to dispossess the plaintiff.

6. That defendant No. 1 has no right, title or interest in the suit property; the power of attorney is void and ineffective against the plaintiff's rights.

7. That the **cause of action** arose on 01.02.2026 when the plaintiff learnt of the forged document and on 05.02.2026 when defendant No. 1 threatened dispossession, and it is continuing.

8. That the suit property is situated at Islamabad within the territorial jurisdiction of this Court.

9. That the value of the suit for jurisdiction is Rs. … and for court fee the relief of declaration is valued at Rs. … and of permanent injunction at Rs. …, on which the requisite court fee has been paid.

**Prayer:** It is respectfully prayed that a decree may kindly be passed in favour of the plaintiff and against the defendants: (a) **declaring** that the plaintiff is the **lawful owner** of the suit property and that the alleged general power of attorney dated 10.12.2025 is **forged, fictitious, void, without lawful authority and ineffective** upon his rights; (b) **cancelling** the said power of attorney (s. 39 SRA) and directing the concerned authority to note the cancellation (if registered); (c) **permanently restraining** defendant No. 1, his agents and servants from interfering with the plaintiff's possession, from dispossessing him, and from alienating or creating any charge on the suit property; (d) **restraining** defendant No. 2 from transferring the suit property on the basis of the said document; (e) costs; (f) any other relief.
:::

**Notes:** (1) **proviso to s. 42 SRA** — because the plaintiff can seek **further relief** (cancellation and injunction), a **bare declaration** would be barred — so claim it; (2) under **s. 42(2)** (2023) the suit must be decided **within six months**; (3) a **temporary injunction** application (O. XXXIX rr. 1–2) is filed with the plaint (Chapter 7.1); (4) fraud is pleaded with **particulars** (O. VI r. 4).

## 5.3 Suit for specific performance of an agreement to sell (Forms No. 47–48)

:::eg Plaint (body)
**SUIT FOR SPECIFIC PERFORMANCE OF AGREEMENT TO SELL DATED 01.03.2025, AND PERMANENT INJUNCTION**

1. That the defendant is the owner of land measuring 8 kanals, Khasra Nos. 112 and 113, Khewat No. 45, Khatooni No. 60, situated in Mouza Tarlai Kalan, Tehsil and District Islamabad, as per the record of rights (Annex-A) (the "suit land").

2. That vide a written **agreement to sell dated 01.03.2025** (Annex-B), attested by two witnesses, the defendant agreed to sell the suit land to the plaintiff for a total consideration of **Rs. 16,000,000/-**, and received **Rs. 4,000,000/-** as **earnest money** in the presence of the witnesses, the balance of Rs. 12,000,000/- being payable at the time of execution and registration of the sale deed on or before **30.09.2025**.

3. That the plaintiff arranged the balance amount and on 25.09.2025 approached the defendant and requested him to appear before the Sub-Registrar for execution and registration of the sale deed, but the defendant avoided doing so.

4. That on 30.09.2025 the plaintiff appeared in the office of the Sub-Registrar, Islamabad, with the balance amount (Annex-C, bank statement showing availability of funds), but the defendant did not appear.

5. That on 05.10.2025 the plaintiff served a legal notice (Annex-D) calling upon the defendant to perform his part; the defendant replied on 20.10.2025 **refusing** to perform and falsely alleging that the agreement had been cancelled.

6. That the plaintiff **has always been and still is ready and willing** to perform his part of the agreement and to pay the balance consideration of Rs. 12,000,000/-, which he is prepared to **deposit in Court** as directed.

7. That the defendant is now trying to **sell the suit land to third parties**, which would cause irreparable loss to the plaintiff.

8. That the **cause of action** arose on 01.03.2025 when the agreement was executed, on 30.09.2025 when the defendant failed to perform, on 20.10.2025 when he refused, and it is continuing.

9. That the suit land is situated within the territorial jurisdiction of this Court.

10. That the suit is within **time** (Limitation Act, Article 113 — three years from the date fixed for performance).

11. That the suit is valued for the purposes of court fee and jurisdiction at **Rs. 16,000,000/-** (the consideration — Court Fees Act s. 7(x)), on which the requisite court fee has been affixed.

**Prayer:** (a) a decree for **specific performance** of the agreement dated 01.03.2025 directing the defendant to **execute and get registered a sale deed** of the suit land in favour of the plaintiff on receipt of the balance consideration of Rs. 12,000,000/-, and to **deliver possession**; and, in case of the defendant's failure, that the sale deed be executed through an **officer of the Court** (O. XXI r. 34); (b) a **permanent injunction** restraining the defendant from alienating or transferring the suit land or creating any charge on it; (c) **in the alternative**, a decree for **refund of the earnest money** of Rs. 4,000,000/- together with **damages** of Rs. … (s. 19 SRA); (d) costs; (e) any other relief.
:::

**Notes:** (1) **readiness and willingness** must be **pleaded and proved** (s. 24(b) SRA; Form 47 para 3); courts commonly direct the plaintiff to **deposit the balance** — failure may be fatal (*Hamood Mehmood v. Mst. Shabana Ishaque*, 2017 SCMR 2022); (2) the agreement must be proved by **two attesting witnesses** (QSO Arts. 17, 79); (3) since 2022, an **agreement to sell immovable property** of Rs. 100 or more is **compulsorily registrable** (Registration Act s. 17(1)(ba)); (4) claim compensation **in the alternative** because dismissal bars a later suit for compensation (s. 29 SRA).

## 5.4 Suit for possession on the basis of title, with mesne profits (SRA s. 8)

:::eg Plaint (key paragraphs)
**SUIT FOR POSSESSION AND RECOVERY OF MESNE PROFITS**

1. That the plaintiff is the owner of the shop No. 3, ground floor, … Plaza, Blue Area, Islamabad (description and boundaries), by virtue of registered sale deed dated … (Annex-A).

2. That on 01.06.2023 the plaintiff permitted the defendant, his relative, to use the shop **as a licensee** without rent, on the understanding that he would vacate on demand.

3. That on 01.01.2026 the plaintiff asked the defendant to vacate; he refused and claimed to be the owner, and has been in **unauthorised occupation** since then.

4. That the shop would fetch a rent of Rs. 150,000/- per month; the plaintiff is entitled to **mesne profits** at that rate from 01.01.2026 till delivery of possession.

5. Cause of action — 01.01.2026 (refusal) and continuing. Jurisdiction — property in Islamabad. Limitation — Article 142 / 139 (twelve years). Valuation — for possession, the market value of the shop Rs. …; for mesne profits, Rs. … to the date of suit; court fee affixed accordingly.

**Prayer:** (a) a decree for **possession** of the shop; (b) **mesne profits** of Rs. … to the date of suit, and future mesne profits at Rs. 150,000/- per month till possession, to be determined under **O. XX r. 12**; (c) costs; (d) any other relief.
:::

**Notes:** (1) the **landlord–tenant** relation, if any, takes the case to the **Rent Controller** under the **Islamabad Rent Restriction Ordinance 2001**; a **licensee** is sued in the civil court; (2) a licensee is **estopped** from denying the licensor's title (QSO Art. 115).

## 5.5 Suit under section 9 SRA (dispossession within six months)

:::eg Plaint (key paragraphs)
**SUIT FOR RECOVERY OF POSSESSION UNDER SECTION 9 OF THE SPECIFIC RELIEF ACT 1877**

1. That the plaintiff was in **peaceful possession** of the agricultural land measuring 4 kanals, Khasra No. 210, Mouza …, Islamabad, since 2015, cultivating it himself (khasra girdawari entries — Annex-A).

2. That on **10.04.2026**, at about 10:00 a.m., the defendant, with five armed companions, entered the land, **forcibly dispossessed** the plaintiff **without his consent** and **otherwise than in due course of law**, and took possession; a report was made to the police the same day (Annex-B).

3. That the plaintiff seeks restoration of possession **notwithstanding any title** the defendant may set up.

4. Cause of action — 10.04.2026. Jurisdiction — land in Islamabad. **Limitation — within six months of dispossession** (Article 3). Valuation and court fee — as per law.

**Prayer:** a decree for **restoration of possession** of the land to the plaintiff, with costs.
:::

**Notes:** no suit lies against the **Government**; **no appeal or review** (revision lies); title is **irrelevant**; the loser may sue on **title** under s. 8 (Civil Law-II, Topic 4). A criminal complaint under the **Illegal Dispossession Act 2005** is a separate remedy.

## 5.6 Suit for cancellation of a document (SRA s. 39)

:::eg Key paragraphs
1. That the plaintiff, an old illiterate pardanashin lady, is the owner of House No. … (description).

2. That defendant No. 1, her nephew, who managed her affairs, got her **thumb impression** on certain papers on 15.08.2025 representing them to be papers for a **pension claim**; she later learnt on 10.02.2026 that the papers were a **gift deed** of the house in his favour, registered on 18.08.2025 (Annex-A).

3. That the gift deed is **void**, obtained by **fraud and misrepresentation**; there was **no declaration, acceptance or delivery of possession** — she remains in possession; it was executed **without independent advice** while defendant No. 1 stood in a position of **active confidence** (QSO Art. 127).

4. That the deed, if left outstanding, may cause her **serious injury** (s. 39 SRA).

5. Cause of action — 10.02.2026 (knowledge). Limitation — Article 91 (three years from knowledge). Valuation — the value of the property; court fee affixed.

**Prayer:** (a) a decree **declaring** the gift deed dated 15.08.2025 (registered 18.08.2025) **void and ineffective** and **cancelling** it, with a direction that a copy of the decree be sent to the **Sub-Registrar** to note the cancellation (s. 39); (b) a **permanent injunction** restraining defendant No. 1 from interfering with her possession or alienating the house; (c) costs.
:::

## 5.7 Suit in the Family Court — dissolution of marriage on khula, dower and maintenance

:::eg Plaint (body)
**IN THE COURT OF THE JUDGE FAMILY COURT, ISLAMABAD (EAST)**

Mst. Ayesha Bibi daughter of … wife of Kamran, resident of … (where she ordinarily resides), Islamabad — **Plaintiff** **Versus** Kamran son of …, resident of … — **Defendant**

**SUIT FOR DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE ON THE BASIS OF KHULA, RECOVERY OF DOWER AND MAINTENANCE**

1. That the parties were married on 12.12.2020 at Islamabad according to Muslim rites; dower was fixed at Rs. 500,000/- **prompt** (unpaid) and 5 tolas of gold ornaments (Nikahnama — Annex-A).

2. That a daughter, Minahil, was born on 05.10.2021 and is in the plaintiff's custody.

3. That the defendant treated the plaintiff with cruelty, beat her and turned her out of his house on 01.03.2025 with the minor; since then he has **not paid any maintenance** to her or the child.

4. That the plaintiff has developed such **hatred and aversion** towards the defendant that it is impossible for the spouses to live together **within the limits prescribed by Allah**; she seeks dissolution on the basis of **khula**.

5. That the plaintiff is entitled to **maintenance** at Rs. 30,000/- per month for herself during the subsistence of the marriage and the iddat, and for the minor at Rs. 20,000/- per month, from 01.03.2025.

6. That the plaintiff **ordinarily resides** at Islamabad within the jurisdiction of this Court (Family Court Rules, r. 6).

**Prayer:** (a) a decree for **dissolution of marriage** by way of **khula**; (b) recovery of **dower** — Rs. 500,000/- and 5 tolas of gold ornaments or their market value; (c) **maintenance** for the plaintiff at Rs. 30,000/- per month from 01.03.2025 till the expiry of iddat; (d) **maintenance** for the minor at Rs. 20,000/- per month from 01.03.2025 till she is married, with annual increase; (e) interim maintenance; (f) costs.

**Annexed (Family Courts Act s. 7):** the **list of witnesses** with addresses, and a **précis of the facts** each witness will depose to; a **schedule of documents** with copies.
:::

**Notes:** in a **khula** suit, if reconciliation fails at the **pre-trial** stage, the Family Court passes a decree **forthwith** and may direct the wife to **restore** the dower she received (Family Courts Act s. 10(4)); the court decides within **six months** (Civil Law-II, Topic 1; verify the section numbers against the Act).

# Chapter 6 · Model written statements

## 6.1 Written statement in the money suit (5.1)

:::eg Written statement
**IN THE COURT OF THE CIVIL JUDGE 1st CLASS, ISLAMABAD (WEST)** — Ahmad Ali versus Bilal Khan — Civil Suit No. 25 of 2026

**WRITTEN STATEMENT ON BEHALF OF THE DEFENDANT**

Respectfully sheweth:—

**Preliminary objections:**

1. That the plaint does not disclose any **cause of action** against the defendant.

2. That the plaintiff has not come to the Court with **clean hands** and has concealed material facts.

3. That the suit is **false, frivolous and vexatious** and is liable to be dismissed with **special costs** under s. 35A CPC.

**On facts:**

1. Para 1 of the plaint is **admitted** to the extent that the parties are known to each other.

2. Para 2 is **incorrect and denied**. The defendant **never borrowed** Rs. 1,500,000/- **or any part of it** from the plaintiff. The amount of the cheque was paid by the plaintiff as the **price of 300 bags of cement** supplied by the defendant to the plaintiff on 08.01.2024 (invoice No. … — to be produced). The alleged receipt-cum-agreement is **forged and fabricated**; the defendant never executed it, and the alleged attesting witnesses are the plaintiff's employees.

3. Para 3 is incorrect and denied. No demand was ever made.

4. Para 4 is admitted to the extent of receipt of the notice; the defendant did not reply as the claim was false. The rest is denied.

5. Para 5 is incorrect and denied. Nothing is due from the defendant.

6. Para 6 is denied. No cause of action has accrued to the plaintiff.

7. Para 7 needs no reply, being a matter of jurisdiction.

8. Para 8 is denied. The suit is **barred by time** as framed, the alleged transaction being of 10.01.2024 and the plaintiff having concealed the true transaction. (Plead limitation only if it truly arises.)

9. Para 9 needs no reply.

It is, therefore, respectfully prayed that the suit of the plaintiff may kindly be **dismissed with costs**, including special costs under s. 35A CPC.

Defendant — Through counsel. **Verification** — Verified on oath at Islamabad on …….2026 that the contents of the written statement are true and correct to my knowledge and belief.
:::

## 6.2 Written statement in the specific performance suit (5.3)

:::eg Key paragraphs
**Preliminary objections:** (1) the plaintiff was **never ready and willing** to perform his part and did not have the balance amount on the date fixed (s. 24(b) SRA); (2) **time was of the essence**; the plaintiff failed to pay by 30.09.2025, so the agreement **stood cancelled** in terms of its clause 5 and the earnest money stood **forfeited**; (3) the suit is barred by **s. 42 SRA / s. 21 SRA** (relief discretionary; compensation adequate); (4) the plaintiff is **estopped** by his conduct.

**On facts:** Para 1 admitted. Para 2 — **admitted** that the agreement was executed and Rs. 4,000,000/- received; **denied** that the date was extendable; time was of the essence (clause 5). Paras 3–4 — **denied**; the plaintiff never approached the defendant; his presence before the Sub-Registrar is **not admitted** and he be put to proof; the bank statement shows only Rs. 2,000,000/- in his account. Para 5 — admitted that notice was received and replied to; the reply was **correct**. Para 6 — **denied**; the plaintiff was and is not ready and willing; in any case **escalation of prices** and the plaintiff's delay disentitle him to the discretionary relief (s. 22 SRA). Para 7 — denied. Paras 8–11 — denied as framed / need no reply.

**Prayer:** dismissal of the suit with costs.
:::

## 6.3 Written statement in the declaration suit (5.2) — defendant's version

:::eg Key paragraphs
**Preliminary objections:** (1) the suit is **not maintainable** in its present form; (2) defendant No. 2 (CDA) is a **necessary party** and statutory notice was not served (if required by its law); (3) the plaintiff has **no cause of action**.

**On facts:** Paras 1–2 — **admitted** that the plaintiff was the transferee of the plot; **denied** that he is still the owner, because he executed a **general power of attorney** dated 10.12.2025 before a **notary public** in favour of defendant No. 1 and received the full price of Rs. … in cash; the document carries the presumption of due execution and authentication (**QSO Art. 95**). Para 3 — admitted that defendant No. 1 applied for transfer; denied that the document is forged. Para 4 — **denied**; the plaintiff's absence abroad is **not admitted**; the plaintiff signed the document in Islamabad in the presence of the notary and two witnesses. Para 5 — **incorrect and denied**; defendant No. 1 never visited the property with armed persons. Paras 6–9 — denied.

**Prayer:** dismissal with costs.
:::

**Lesson:** each paragraph is **admitted, denied (with the defendant's own version), or not admitted** — never "incorrect as stated" alone; and every **new fact** (payment of price, time of the essence, notarised execution) is **specially pleaded** (O. VIII r. 2).

# Chapter 7 · Common applications

## 7.1 Application for temporary injunction (O. XXXIX rr. 1–2 read with s. 151 CPC)

:::eg Format
**APPLICATION UNDER ORDER XXXIX RULES 1 AND 2 READ WITH SECTION 151 CPC FOR GRANT OF TEMPORARY INJUNCTION**

1. That the applicant / plaintiff has filed the accompanying suit for declaration and permanent injunction, the contents of which may be read as part of this application.

2. That the applicant has a **prima facie case** — he is the registered owner in possession and the power of attorney relied upon by respondent No. 1 is forged (passport entries show he was abroad).

3. That the **balance of convenience** lies in his favour — he is in possession; the respondents would suffer no loss if restrained until the decision of the suit.

4. That the applicant would suffer **irreparable loss**, not compensable in money, if respondent No. 1 transfers the property or dispossesses him.

It is, therefore, prayed that the respondents be **restrained** from transferring, alienating or creating any charge on the suit property and from interfering with the applicant's possession **till the final decision of the suit**; and that an **ad interim** injunction be granted meanwhile (O. XXXIX r. 3 — with reasons and notice).

Supported by an **affidavit** of the applicant.
:::

## 7.2 Application for rejection of plaint (O. VII r. 11 CPC)

"The plaint does not disclose a cause of action / is barred by law (Limitation Act, Article …; s. 11 CPC; proviso to s. 42 SRA), as is evident from the plaint itself and the documents annexed; it may be **rejected** under O. VII r. 11(a) and (d) CPC." — The court decides on the **plaint and its annexures** only, assuming the averments to be true.

## 7.3 Application for amendment of plaint (O. VI r. 17 CPC)

"Through inadvertence the khasra number of the suit land was written as 112 instead of 121 in para 1 and the prayer clause; the amendment is necessary for determining the real controversy, does not change the nature of the suit, and causes no prejudice to the defendant; the amended plaint is annexed." — Supported by an affidavit; the court allows on just terms.

# Chapter 8 · From pleadings to issues to judgment

## 8.1 Framing issues — O. XIV

1. **When issues arise (r. 1):** where a **material proposition of fact or law** is **affirmed by one party and denied by the other**; each such proposition forms the subject of a **distinct issue**.
2. **Material propositions** are those which the plaintiff must allege to show a **right to sue**, or the defendant must allege to constitute his **defence**.
3. **Sources (r. 3):** the **pleadings**, the **statements of the parties or their counsel** (O. X), and the **documents** produced.
4. **Issues of law first (r. 2):** where the case can be disposed of on an issue of **law** relating to **jurisdiction** or a **bar** created by law, the court may try it first.
5. **Form:** a **question**, beginning with "**Whether**", stating the **onus** — "**OPP**" (onus on the plaintiff) or "**OPD**" (onus on the defendant); the last issue is always "**Relief**".

:::eg Issues for the specific-performance suit (5.3 / 6.2)
1. Whether the defendant executed the agreement to sell dated 01.03.2025 for Rs. 16,000,000/- and received Rs. 4,000,000/- as earnest money? **OPP** (largely admitted — reframe as below if admitted)
2. Whether the plaintiff has always been and still is ready and willing to perform his part of the agreement? **OPP**
3. Whether time was of the essence of the contract and the agreement stood cancelled on the plaintiff's failure to pay by 30.09.2025, with forfeiture of the earnest money? **OPD**
4. Whether the plaintiff is entitled to a decree for specific performance, or in the alternative to refund of the earnest money and damages? **OPP**
5. Whether the suit is not maintainable in its present form? **OPD**
6. Whether the plaintiff is estopped by his words and conduct to file the suit? **OPD**
7. **Relief.**
:::

:::eg Issues for the money suit (5.1 / 6.1)
1. Whether the defendant obtained a loan of Rs. 1,500,000/- from the plaintiff on 10.01.2024, repayable by 10.07.2024, and executed the receipt-cum-agreement (Annex-A)? **OPP**
2. Whether the amount of the cheque was paid as the price of cement supplied by the defendant, and the receipt is forged? **OPD**
3. Whether the suit is barred by time? **OPD**
4. Whether the suit is false and vexatious and the defendant is entitled to special costs under s. 35A CPC? **OPD**
5. **Relief.**
:::

## 8.2 Writing a judgment — O. XX rr. 4, 5

1. **Contents (r. 4(2)):** a judgment of a court other than a Small Cause Court shall contain a **concise statement of the case**, the **points for determination**, the **decision thereon**, and the **reasons** for such decision.
2. **Issue-wise (r. 5):** the court shall state its **finding or decision, with reasons, upon each separate issue**, unless the finding on one or more is sufficient for the decision.
3. **Pronouncement (rr. 1–3)** — in open court, dated and signed; the **decree** follows within **fifteen days** in the prescribed form, specifying the relief and costs (rr. 6–7).

:::eg Skeleton of a civil judgment
**IN THE COURT OF THE CIVIL JUDGE 1st CLASS, ISLAMABAD (WEST)** — Civil Suit No. 25 of 2026 — Ahmad Ali versus Bilal Khan — Date of institution … — Date of decision …

**JUDGMENT**

1. **The case in brief** — the plaintiff's claim (two or three sentences); the defendant's defence; the **issues** framed on (date) (reproduce them).

2. **Evidence** — the plaintiff examined PW-1 (himself), PW-2 and PW-3 (attesting witnesses) and produced Exh. P-1 to P-4; the defendant examined DW-1 and produced Exh. D-1; arguments heard.

3. **Issue-wise findings** — for each issue: the onus; the evidence for and against (oral and documentary); its **admissibility** and **weight** (e.g., "Exh. P-1 is a document of a financial obligation; both attesting witnesses have been produced and their evidence is consistent — Art. 79 QSO is complied with"; "the defendant's version of a cement sale is not supported by any invoice or delivery record; presumption under Art. 129(g) arises as he withheld the invoice"); the **finding** — "Issue No. 1 is decided in **favour of the plaintiff**".

4. **Relief** — "In view of my findings on Issues 1 to 4, the suit is **decreed** for Rs. 1,500,000/- with costs. Decree sheet be prepared. File be consigned to the record room after completion."

(Signed) Civil Judge 1st Class, Islamabad (West). Announced in open court on ….
:::

# Chapter 9 · Checklists and last-night revision

## 9.1 Plaint checklist (O. VII)

:::recap Plaint — 15 items
1. Court (lowest competent; territorial; pecuniary) and suit number.
2. Title — parties with parentage, age, occupation, address; minor through next friend; Government through Secretary; company through CEO.
3. Nature of suit and provision in the heading.
4. Material facts — chronological, numbered, figures for dates and sums.
5. Particulars of fraud, misrepresentation, undue influence (O. VI r. 4).
6. Description of immovable property — boundaries, khasra / plot numbers (r. 3).
7. Plaintiff's interest and defendant's liability (rr. 4–5).
8. Demand / notice and refusal; s. 80 notice against Government.
9. Cause of action and when it arose (r. 1(e)).
10. Jurisdiction (r. 1(f)).
11. Limitation — and exemption grounds if late (r. 6).
12. Valuation for jurisdiction and court fee; court fee paid (r. 1(i)).
13. Relief — specific, alternative, separate (rr. 7–8); avoid bare declaration (s. 42 proviso).
14. Signature of party and counsel (O. VI r. 14); verification by paragraphs (r. 15).
15. Annexures — documents sued on and list (rr. 14–18), copies and summons forms (r. 9), address for service (r. 19), list of legal representatives (r. 26), vakalatnama, affidavit; TI application if needed.
:::

## 9.2 Written statement checklist (O. VIII)

:::recap Written statement — 12 items
1. Title and suit number as in the plaint; "Written statement on behalf of defendant No. …".
2. Filed within the time allowed (ordinarily 30 days — r. 1).
3. Preliminary objections — only those arising: cause of action, locus standi, limitation, jurisdiction, res judicata, s. 10, O. II r. 2, non-joinder, valuation, s. 80 notice, s. 42 proviso, estoppel.
4. Parawise reply — each paragraph admitted, denied with own version, or not admitted (rr. 3–5).
5. No evasive denial — "that sum or any part thereof" (r. 4).
6. New facts specially pleaded — fraud, payment, release, performance, limitation, illegality (r. 2).
7. Separate grounds separately (r. 7).
8. Set-off with particulars and court fee (r. 6); counter-claim — separate suit or cross-claim with court fee.
9. Additional pleas arising after suit (r. 8).
10. Prayer — dismissal with costs; special costs s. 35A.
11. Signature and verification (O. VI rr. 14–15).
12. Address for service and list of legal representatives (rr. 11–13); list of documents (O. VIII r. 1-A practice / O. XIII).
:::

## 9.3 The 25 points of Topic 3

:::recap Revision sheet
1. Pleading = plaint or written statement (O. VI r. 1); later pleadings only with leave (O. VIII r. 9).
2. Objects — issues, notice, no surprise, narrowing, record; *secundum allegata et probata*.
3. O. VI r. 2 — material facts, not law, not evidence, concise, numbered, figures.
4. Material facts vs particulars; further and better particulars (r. 5).
5. Fraud etc. with particulars (r. 4); condition precedent (r. 6); no departure (r. 7); bare denial of contract (r. 8).
6. Effect of documents, malice, notice, implied contracts, presumptions (rr. 9–13).
7. Signing (r. 14) and verification by paragraphs, own knowledge vs information (r. 15).
8. Striking out (r. 16); amendment at any stage — not new cause, not time-barred claim, not withdrawing admission (r. 17); 14 days (r. 18).
9. O. VII r. 1 — nine particulars (court, parties, disability, cause of action, jurisdiction, relief, set-off, valuation).
10. rr. 2–8 — precise amount, property description, representative capacity, defendant's interest, limitation exemption, specific / alternative / separate reliefs.
11. Papers — documents and lists (rr. 14–18), copies (r. 9), address (rr. 19–21), legal representatives (r. 26), s. 80 notice.
12. Valuation — jurisdiction and court fee; ad valorem vs fixed; s. 7 CFA; s. 149 CPC.
13. Return (r. 10) vs rejection (r. 11 — four grounds; fresh plaint allowed — r. 13).
14. O. VIII r. 1 — first hearing / 30 days ordinarily; r. 10 — judgment for failure.
15. r. 2 new facts; r. 3 specific denial; r. 4 no evasive denial; r. 5 non-denial = admission.
16. Admit / deny with own version / not admit — never only "incorrect as stated".
17. Preliminary objections — the usual eleven; plead only those that arise.
18. Set-off (r. 6) — money suit, ascertained, legally recoverable, same character, first hearing; equitable set-off; no counter-claim rule in the Islamabad CPC.
19. Structure — heading, body, cause of action, jurisdiction, limitation, valuation, prayer, signatures, verification, annexures.
20. Money suit — Art. 57; attesting witnesses (QSO Arts. 17, 79); O. XXXVII for negotiable instruments.
21. Declaration — further relief (s. 42 proviso); six-month decision (s. 42(2)); TI application.
22. Specific performance — readiness and willingness; deposit; Art. 113; alternative refund; s. 29 SRA.
23. Possession (s. 8; Art. 142) vs s. 9 (six months; no appeal); cancellation (s. 39; Art. 91; copy to Sub-Registrar).
24. Family Court plaint — residence of wife; list of witnesses and précis; khula on pre-trial failure of reconciliation.
25. Issues — affirmed and denied material propositions; "Whether … OPP / OPD"; law issues first; judgment — concise case, points, decision, reasons, issue-wise (O. XX rr. 4–5).
:::
