# How to use this knowledge base

## What Topic 3 covers

The Limitation Act 1908 has five Parts and a long **First Schedule**:

1. **Part I** — preliminary and definitions (ss. 1–2);
2. **Part II** — limitation of suits, appeals and applications: the bar (s. 3), court closed (s. 4), extension (s. 5), legal disability (ss. 6–8), continuous running (s. 9), trusts (s. 10), foreign contracts (s. 11);
3. **Part III** — computation of the period: exclusions (ss. 12–16), death (s. 17), fraud (s. 18), acknowledgment (s. 19), part-payment (s. 20), agents and joint contractors (s. 21), new parties (s. 22), continuing wrongs (s. 23), special damage (s. 24), Gregorian calendar (s. 25);
4. **Part IV** — acquisition of easements by prescription (ss. 26–27) (s. 28 omitted in 1995);
5. **Part V** — savings (s. 29);
6. **First Schedule** — **183 Articles** fixing the periods: **suits** (Arts. 1–149), **appeals** (Arts. 150–157) and **applications** (Arts. 158–183).

The bare Act **may** be consulted in the exam. So the knowledge base concentrates on **principles**, **conditions** and **case law** — the things the bare Act does not tell you — and on the **key Articles** you must find quickly.

:::ict Pakistani features
1. **Section 28** (extinguishment of the right to property at the end of the limitation period for possession) and **Article 144** (adverse possession — 12 years) were **omitted by Act II of 1995**, after the Shariat Appellate Bench held that acquiring title by adverse possession is repugnant to the Injunctions of Islam (*Maqbool Ahmad v. Hakoomat-e-Pakistan*, 1991 SCMR 2063).
2. **Section 5** applies to an "appeal or application for **a revision** or a review of judgment or for leave to appeal" and to other applications to which it is made applicable by any enactment — it does **not** apply to **suits**.
3. **Section 15(2)** (time of a statutory notice, e.g., s. 80 CPC, excluded) was **added by Act XIII of 1991**.
4. **Article 182** (execution) was **omitted in 1972**; execution applications fall under **Article 181** (three years), read with **s. 48 CPC** (six years).
5. **Article 149** — suits by or on behalf of the **Federal Government** or a Provincial Government: **sixty years**.
6. **Article 103 / 104** — prompt and deferred **dower** (Topic 1).
:::

:::tip The pattern
Six things are asked again and again: the **policy** of limitation; the court's **duty under s. 3**; **sufficient cause** under s. 5; **s. 9**; the **exclusions** (ss. 12–15); and **acknowledgment** (s. 19). Master Chapters 1–6.
