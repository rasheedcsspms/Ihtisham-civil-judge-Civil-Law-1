# How to use this knowledge base

## What is asked

Recent Civil Judge papers asked: "**Minto-Morley Reforms** were a tool of change in the political system of India" (LHC 2023); "**Pakistan is a federation in form and unitary in spirit** — strengths and weaknesses of the Federation" (2023); "the root causes of **corruption** in Pakistan" (2023); "the **genuine factors** responsible for the demand for a **separate homeland**" (2023); "**Population explosion** is a constant threat to the economy" (2020); and dozens of MCQs on the Pakistan Movement (Lucknow Pact, Jallianwala Bagh, Chauri Chaura, Round Table Conferences, Radcliffe Award) and **geography** (rivers of Balochistan, Karakoram, Khojak tunnel, Tirich Mir).

:::tip Writing a Pakistan Studies essay
Introduction with a thesis → historical background → analysis under 6–8 headings with **facts and figures** → the constitutional / legal position (quote Articles) → comparison (India, other federations) → **recommendations** (concrete, numbered) → conclusion. Facts and dates earn marks; avoid emotional language.
:::
