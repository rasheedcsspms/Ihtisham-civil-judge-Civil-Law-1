# How to use this knowledge base

## What Topic 2 covers

1. **Oral evidence** — the rule of **direct** evidence and its exceptions; *shahada ala al-shahadah*.
2. **Documentary evidence** — primary and secondary evidence, **electronic** documents, proof of **signature, handwriting and execution**, **attestation**, public and private documents, **certified copies**.
3. **Presumptions** as to documents — certified copies, **powers of attorney**, documents thirty years old.
4. The **exclusion of oral** by documentary evidence and its six provisos.
5. Facts that **need not be proved** — judicial notice and admissions; **estoppel**.
6. **Burden of proof** and the **presumptions** of fact and law, including legitimacy.
7. **Examination of witnesses** — order, leading questions, cross-examination, the **hostile** witness, **impeaching credit**, corroboration, **refreshing memory**, the judge's powers.
8. **Improper admission** (Art. 162), **decision on oath** (Art. 163) and evidence through **modern devices** (Art. 164).
