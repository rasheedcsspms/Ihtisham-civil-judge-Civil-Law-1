
# Chapter 1 · Foundations

## 1.1 Hadd and ta'zir

{w:22,39,39}
|Point|Hadd (plural hudood)|Ta'zir|
|Meaning|"Punishment **ordained by the Holy Quran or Sunnah**" (s. 2 of each Ordinance)|"Any punishment **other than hadd**" (s. 2)|
|Fixed?|**Fixed** in kind and quantity — no court, ruler or victim can **increase, reduce, pardon or compound** it|**Discretionary** — the State fixes it and the court chooses within limits|
|Proof|**Strict**: confession before the court, or a fixed number of **Muslim adult male** witnesses of proven integrity (**tazkiyah-al-shuhood**)|Ordinary evidence (Qanun-e-Shahadat 1984)|

:::maxim Idra'u al-hudood bi al-shubuhat
"Avert the hudood by doubts." Because hadd is severe and fixed, any doubt — in the evidence, the circumstances or the law — takes the case out of hadd; the offender may still be punished with **ta'zir** on ordinary evidence.
:::

## 1.2 Tazkiyah-al-shuhood

"The mode of inquiry adopted by a Court to **satisfy itself as to the credibility of a witness**" — that the witnesses are **truthful persons** who **abstain from major sins (kabair)**. The court inquires, openly or secretly, into the character of the witnesses (from people who know them) before accepting their evidence for hadd. If the accused is a **non-Muslim**, the witnesses may be **non-Muslims**.

## 1.4 The Protection of Women (Criminal Laws Amendment) Act 2006

The 2006 Act (**Act VI of 2006**) reshaped the Zina and Qazf Ordinances:

1. **Rape** (zina-bil-jabr) was **removed** from the Zina Ordinance and placed in the **PPC** (ss. 375–376 — Topic 2), so that a rape victim who cannot produce four witnesses is **no longer exposed** to prosecution for zina.
2. **Ta'zir** forms of zina were removed; consensual sex outside marriage proved by ordinary evidence became **fornication** (s. 496B PPC) — with false accusation of fornication punishable (s. 496C).
3. Zina and fornication cases can begin **only on a complaint in court** (CrPC ss. **203A, 203C**) — the police cannot register them; a **zina** complaint requires the complainant and **four eye-witnesses** to be examined on oath at the very outset.
4. **No conversion** — a zina complaint or a rape allegation can **never be converted** into fornication, and vice versa (Zina Ordinance s. **5A**).
5. A woman accused of zina is investigated only by an officer **not below SP** and **not arrested without the court's permission** (CrPC s. 156B).
6. **Qazf** — the ta'zir provisions were omitted; where a **zina complaint fails**, the court may convict the complainant of **qazf** without further proof (Qazf s. 6(2)); false accusation in other forms falls under **defamation** (PPC).
7. **Lian** was added as a ground for dissolution of marriage — DMMA s. 2(viia) (Civil Law-II, Topic 1).

# Chapter 2 · The Offence of Zina (Enforcement of Hudood) Ordinance 1979

## 2.1 Definitions — s. 2

1. **Adult** — a **male of 18**, a **female of 16**, or one who has attained **puberty**.
2. **Confession** — "notwithstanding any judgment of any court to the contrary, an **oral statement**, **explicitly** admitting the commission of the offence of zina, **voluntarily** made by the accused **before a Court of Session** having jurisdiction, or on receipt of a summons under **s. 203A CrPC**" (2006).
3. **Hadd** — punishment ordained by the Holy Quran or Sunnah.
4. **Muhsan** — (i) a **Muslim adult man**, not insane, who has had sexual intercourse with a **Muslim adult woman** who was **married to him** and not insane at the time; or (ii) a Muslim adult woman, not insane, who has had intercourse with a Muslim adult man **married to her**, not insane.

## 2.2 Zina — s. 4

"A man and a woman are said to commit **zina** if they **wilfully** have sexual intercourse **without being married** to each other."

## 2.3 Zina liable to hadd — s. 5

1. **Ingredients:** zina committed by a man (or woman) who is an **adult**, **not insane**, with a woman (man) to whom he is **not, and does not suspect himself to be, married**.
2. **Punishment (s. 5(2)):** if **muhsan** — **stoning to death** at a public place; if **not muhsan** — **whipping of one hundred stripes** at a public place.
3. **Confirmation (s. 5(3)):** not executed until **confirmed** by the court to which an appeal lies (the **Federal Shariat Court**); until then, a whipping convict is treated as if sentenced to **simple imprisonment**.

## 2.4 No conversion — s. 5A (2006)

No complaint of zina under s. 5 read with s. 203A CrPC, and **no case in which an allegation of rape is made**, shall at any stage be **converted** into a complaint of **fornication** (s. 496B PPC); and no complaint of fornication shall be converted into one of zina.

## 2.5 Proof of zina liable to hadd — s. 8

1. **(a)** the accused makes a **confession** before a court of competent jurisdiction; **or**
2. **(b)** at least **four Muslim adult male witnesses**, about whom the court is satisfied (tazkiyah-al-shuhood) that they are **truthful** and **abstain from major sins**, give evidence as **eye-witnesses of the act of penetration** necessary to the offence.

## 2.6 Cases in which hadd shall not be enforced — s. 9

1. Where proved **only by confession**, hadd (or the part not yet executed) is **not enforced** if the convict **retracts** his confession before execution.
2. Where proved **only by testimony**, hadd is not enforced if any witness **resiles** before execution so as to reduce the eye-witnesses to **fewer than four**.

## 2.7 Execution of stoning — s. 17

Such of the **witnesses** who deposed as are available start the stoning; while it is carried on, the convict **may be shot dead**, whereupon stoning and shooting stop.

## 2.8 Procedure — ss. 20, 21; CrPC

1. The **CrPC** applies mutatis mutandis (s. 20(1)).
2. Triable by a **Court of Session** — not a s. 30 Magistrate — ordinarily at the **tehsil headquarters**; **appeal to the Federal Shariat Court**.
3. CrPC provisions on **confirmation** of death sentences apply (s. 20(2)); ss. 391(3) and 393 CrPC (limits on whipping) do not apply to hadd whipping (s. 20(4)).
4. **Presiding officer** must be a **Muslim** — or may be non-Muslim if the accused is non-Muslim (s. 21).
5. **Complaint only** — no court takes cognizance of s. 5 except on a **complaint in court**; the court at once examines on oath the complainant and **at least four Muslim adult male eye-witnesses** of **penetration**; if sufficient ground, it **summons** the accused (no arrest); otherwise it **dismisses** the complaint with reasons (CrPC s. 203A).
6. **Consequence of failure** — a complainant who cannot produce four witnesses, or a witness found to have given false evidence, may be punished for **qazf** without further proof (Qazf s. 6(2)).

# Chapter 3 · The Offence of Qazf (Enforcement of Hadd) Ordinance 1979

## 3.1 Qazf — s. 3

"Whoever by **words** either spoken or intended to be read, or by **signs** or by **visible representations**, makes or publishes an **imputation of zina** concerning any person **intending to harm**, or knowing or having reason to believe that such imputation will harm, the **reputation**, or **hurt the feelings**, of such person, is said … to commit qazf."

1. *Explanation 1:* imputing zina to a **deceased** person may be qazf if it would harm him if living and hurt his family.
2. *Explanation 2:* an imputation in the form of an **alternative** or **ironically** may be qazf.
3. *First Exception:* imputation of **truth** made or published for the **public good**.
4. *Second Exception:* an accusation of zina preferred **in good faith** to a person having **lawful authority** — **but not** where (a) a complainant accuses another of zina in a court but **fails to produce four witnesses**; (b) a witness is found by the court to have given **false evidence** of zina; (c) a complainant has made a **false accusation** of zina-bil-jabr.

## 3.2 Qazf liable to hadd — s. 5

Whoever, being an **adult**, **intentionally** and **without ambiguity** commits qazf of **zina liable to hadd** against a **particular person** who is a **muhsan** and **capable of performing sexual intercourse**. *Muhsan here* — a **sane, adult Muslim** who has had **no** sexual intercourse, or only with his or her **lawfully wedded spouse**. *Explanation 2:* calling a person **illegitimate**, or refusing to recognise a person as legitimate, is qazf liable to hadd against his **mother**.

## 3.3 Proof — s. 6

1. **(a)** the accused **confesses** before a court of competent jurisdiction;
2. **(b)** the accused commits qazf **in the presence of the court**; or
3. **(c)** at least **two Muslim adult male witnesses**, other than the victim, satisfying tazkiyah-al-shuhood, give **direct evidence** of the qazf (non-Muslims where the accused is non-Muslim); the complainant's statement is recorded **first**.
4. **s. 6(2) (2006):** a court **dismissing a zina complaint** under s. 203A CrPC, or **acquitting** an accused of zina under s. 5, if satisfied that qazf liable to hadd has been committed, shall **not require any proof** of qazf and shall **pass sentence** under s. 7.

## 3.4 Punishment — s. 7

1. **Whipping of eighty stripes** (s. 7(1)).
2. After conviction for qazf liable to hadd, the convict's **evidence shall not be admissible** in any court of law (s. 7(2)) — a lasting disqualification.
3. Not executed until **confirmed** by the appellate court; meanwhile treated as in simple imprisonment (subject to bail and suspension).

## 3.5 Who can complain — s. 8

Proceedings begin **only on a complaint lodged in a court** by (a) the person defamed, if **alive**, or his authorised person; (b) if **dead**, any of his **ascendants or descendants**.

## 3.6 Hadd not imposed or enforced — s. 9

Hadd is **not imposed** (a) where the qazf is against a **descendant** of the accuser; (b) where the victim-complainant **dies during the proceedings**; (c) where the imputation is **proved true**. It is **not enforced** where, before execution, the complainant **withdraws** the allegation, or says the accused falsely confessed or a witness deposed falsely (s. 9(2)).

## 3.7 Lian — s. 14

1. **When** — a **husband accuses his wife** (who is muhsan) **before a court** of zina, and the wife **denies** it.
2. **Procedure** — the husband swears **four times** by Allah that he is truthful in his accusation and the **fifth** time invokes **Allah's curse** on himself if he is lying; the wife swears **four times** that he is a liar and the **fifth** time invokes **Allah's wrath** on herself if he is truthful (Surah an-Nur, 24:6–9).
3. **Effect** — the court passes an order **dissolving the marriage**, which operates as a **decree for dissolution**; **no appeal** lies.
4. Lian is also a ground under **DMMA s. 2(viia)**; the Family Court may proceed on it.

## 3.8 Procedure — ss. 17, 18

The CrPC applies; trial by the **Court of Session** at the tehsil headquarters or where the accusing husband resides; appeal to the **Federal Shariat Court**; Chapter XXIX CrPC (suspension, remission, commutation) does **not** apply to hadd for qazf; presiding officer a **Muslim**.

# Chapter 4 · The Offences against Property (Enforcement of Hudood) Ordinance 1979 — KPHC 2026

## 4.1 Definitions — s. 2

1. **Adult** — a person who has attained **18 years or puberty**.
2. **Authorised medical officer** — a medical officer authorised by Government.
3. **Hadd** — punishment ordained by the Holy Quran or Sunnah.
4. **Hirz** — "an **arrangement made for the custody of property**".
5. **Imprisonment for life** — **imprisonment till death**.
6. **Nisab** — as laid down in s. 6.
7. **Ta'zir** — any punishment other than hadd; other terms bear their PPC / CrPC meaning.

The Ordinance **overrides** other laws (s. 3).

## 4.2 The concept of hirz (KPHC 2026)

![Hirz — the custody that turns theft into hadd theft](hirz_map.png)

### Meaning

**Hirz** (literally "a safe or protected place") is "an **arrangement made for the custody of property**" (s. 2(d)). In Islamic law, the hand is cut only for taking property out of **protection** — a person who leaves property unguarded has himself exposed it, and the taker's act, though theft, lacks the gravity that justifies the fixed penalty. Hirz is therefore an **essential ingredient** of theft liable to hadd (s. 5: "commits, **from any hirz**, theft …").

### What is hirz — Explanation 1

"Property placed (a) in a **house**, **whether its door is closed or not**, or (b) in an **almirah** or a **box** or **other container**, or (c) in the **custody of a person**, **whether he is paid for such custody or not**, is said to be in hirz."

So the Ordinance recognises the **two classical kinds**:

{w:26,74}
|Kind|Meaning and examples|
|**Hirz bil-makan** (by place)|A place **meant** for keeping property — a **house** (door open or shut), shop, store, almirah, safe, trunk, box, locked vehicle|
|**Hirz bil-hafiz** (by guard)|Property in the **custody of a person** — a watchman, a shopkeeper sitting with his goods, a person carrying his purse or wearing his jewellery, a guard over cattle — **paid or unpaid**|

### One hirz or many — Explanation 2

"If a **single family** is living in a house, the **entire house** will constitute a **single hirz**; but if **two or more families** are living in one house **severally**, the portion in the occupation of **each family** will constitute a **separate hirz**." This matters for the **nisab** — value taken from different rooms of **one** family's house is **added together**; from **two families'** portions, it is not (s. 6, *illus. (a)*).

### Consequences of the hirz requirement

1. **No hirz — no hadd:** taking property lying **openly** in a public place or an unguarded field is theft liable only to **ta'zir** (s. 13), punished under the **PPC** (s. 14).
2. **Access to the hirz excludes hadd:** a **guest** stealing from his **host's house** (s. 10(b)), a **servant or employee** stealing from the hirz of his master **to which he is allowed access** (s. 10(c)) — the property was not "protected" **against him**.
3. **Each hirz is separate** — several thefts from one hirz, each below nisab, are **not** added (s. 6, Explanation and *illus. (b)*).
4. **Entering the hirz** — where several persons commit hadd theft, hadd is imposed on all **who have entered the hirz**, if each one's equal share reaches nisab, whether or not each moved the property (s. 8).
5. **Theft from the person** (pick-pocketing, snatching) — the person is a hafiz; a **surreptitious** taking of nisab-value property from his custody may be hadd theft, but open snatching is not "surreptitious" and falls outside hadd (see s. 5, Explanation 2).

## 4.3 Theft liable to hadd — s. 5

> "Whoever, **being an adult**, **surreptitiously** commits, **from any hirz**, theft of property of the **value of the nisab or more**, **not being stolen property**, **knowing** that it is or is likely to be of the value of the nisab or more is, subject to the provisions of this Ordinance, said to commit theft liable to hadd."

### Ingredients

1. **Theft** as defined in the PPC (s. 378) — dishonest taking of movable property out of another's possession without consent (s. 2(g));
2. by an **adult** (18 years or puberty);
3. **surreptitiously** — the thief believes the victim **does not know** of his action. *Explanation 2:* by **day** (which includes **one hour before sunrise and two hours after sunset**) the secrecy must **continue till completion**; by **night**, it need not continue **after commencement**;
4. **from a hirz**;
5. property of the value of **nisab or more**;
6. **not "stolen property"** — taking from a thief is not hadd theft (but property **criminally misappropriated** or the subject of **criminal breach of trust** is not "stolen property" for this purpose — Explanation 1);
7. **knowledge** that the value is or is likely to be **nisab or more**.

## 4.4 Nisab — s. 6

The nisab is **4.457 grams of gold** (four decimal four five seven), or other property of equivalent value, **at the time of the theft**. *Explanation:* thefts in **more than one transaction**, or from **more than one hirz**, each below nisab, do **not** make hadd theft even if they add up to nisab.

## 4.5 Proof — s. 7 (KPHC 2026)

Proof of theft liable to hadd shall be in **one** of two forms:

1. **(a)** the accused **pleads guilty** of the commission of theft liable to hadd; **or**
2. **(b)** at least **two Muslim adult male witnesses**, **other than the victim**, about whom the court is satisfied, having regard to **tazkiyah-al-shuhood**, that they are **truthful** persons and **abstain from major sins** (kabair), give evidence as **eye-witnesses of the occurrence**.

*Provisos:* if the accused is **non-Muslim**, the eye-witnesses may be **non-Muslims**; the **statement of the victim** (or his authorised person) shall be recorded **before** the statements of the eye-witnesses.

If either form of proof is **not available**, the offence is theft liable to **ta'zir** (s. 13) and may be proved by ordinary evidence.

## 4.6 Several offenders — s. 8

Where hadd theft is committed by **more than one person**, and the aggregate value is such that, **divided equally** among those **who entered the hirz**, each share **amounts to or exceeds nisab**, hadd is imposed on **all who entered the hirz**, whether or not each moved the property.

## 4.7 Punishment — s. 9

1. **First** hadd theft — **amputation of the right hand from the joint of the wrist**.
2. **Second** — **amputation of the left foot up to the ankle**.
3. **Third or subsequent** — **imprisonment for life** (till death); the appellate court may release him on terms if satisfied that he is **sincerely penitent** (s. 9(5)).

## 4.8 The process of implementation (KPHC 2026)

![Theft liable to hadd — from complaint to execution](hadd_theft_flow.png)

1. **Trial** by a **Court of Session** (not a s. 30 Magistrate), ordinarily at the **tehsil headquarters**; presiding officer a **Muslim** (ss. 24, 25).
2. **Proof** strictly under s. 7 — plea of guilty, or two qualified eye-witnesses after **tazkiyah**; victim's statement first.
3. **Sentence** of amputation under s. 9.
4. **Appeal and confirmation** — the punishment **shall not be executed unless it is confirmed** by the court to which an appeal lies — the **Federal Shariat Court** (s. 9(4); s. 24 proviso); until confirmed and executed, the convict is treated as in **simple imprisonment**.
5. **No remission or commutation** — Chapter XXIX CrPC (suspension, remission, commutation of sentences) does **not** apply to punishments under ss. 9 and 17 (s. 24(4)).
6. **Execution** by an **authorised medical officer** (s. 9(6)) — if he is of the opinion that amputation **may cause death**, execution is **postponed** until the apprehension ceases (s. 9(7)).
7. **Cases where hadd is not enforced** (s. 11) — retraction of the confession; a witness resiling so that fewer than two remain; the victim **withdrawing** the allegation or saying the confession or evidence was false; or the offender's **left hand, left thumb, two fingers of the left hand, or right foot** being missing or unserviceable — then **retrial** (after retraction) or **ta'zir** on the evidence (s. 11(2), (3)).
8. **Return of property** (s. 12) — the stolen property, or what it was converted into, is returned to the victim from whoever holds it; if lost or consumed while with the offender and **hadd is enforced**, the offender pays **no compensation**.
9. **In practice** — owing to the strict proof required and the doctrine of doubt, sentences of amputation have rarely been passed and none is reported to have been executed; theft cases are decided as **ta'zir** under the PPC.

## 4.9 Cases in which hadd shall not be imposed — s. 10

1. **(a)** offender and victim are related as **spouses**; **ascendants** or **descendants** (paternal or maternal); **paternal or maternal uncles and aunts**; **brothers or sisters or their children**;
2. **(b)** a **guest** steals from the house of his **host**;
3. **(c)** a **servant or employee** steals from the hirz of his master to which he is **allowed access**;
4. **(d)** the property is **wild grass, fish, bird, dog, pig, intoxicant, musical instrument**, or **perishable foodstuff** for whose preservation there is no provision;
5. **(e)** the offender has a **share** in the property and, after deducting it, the value is **below nisab**;
6. **(f)** a **creditor** steals his debtor's property and, after deducting the debt, the value is below nisab;
7. **(g)** theft under **ikrah** (putting a person in fear of injury to the person, property or honour of himself or another) or **iztirar** (apprehension of **death from extreme hunger or thirst**);
8. **(h)** the offender, **before apprehension**, out of **repentance**, **returns** the property and **surrenders** to the authorities.

## 4.10 Theft liable to ta'zir — ss. 13, 14

Theft which is **not liable to hadd**, or for which s. 7 proof is **not available**, or for which hadd may not be imposed or enforced, is liable to **ta'zir** — the punishment provided for **theft in the PPC** (ss. 379–382).

## 4.11 Haraabah — ss. 15 to 20

1. **Definition (s. 15)** — when one or more persons, **armed or not**, make a **show of force** for the purpose of **taking away the property** of another and **attack** him or cause **wrongful restraint** or put him in **fear of death or hurt** (the Islamic counterpart of robbery and dacoity; Quran 5:33).
2. **Proof (s. 16)** — as in s. 7.
3. **Punishment (s. 17)** — (1) **no murder and no property taken** — **whipping up to 30 stripes** and rigorous imprisonment until **sincerely penitent**, not less than **three years**; (2) **hurt caused**, no property taken — in addition, punishment for the hurt; (3) **property of nisab value taken**, no murder — **amputation of the right hand from the wrist and the left foot from the ankle** (each offender's share must reach nisab; if the left hand or right foot is missing — RI up to 14 years and 30 stripes); (4) **murder committed** — **death as hadd**.
4. **Confirmation and execution** — as for theft; ss. 10 and 11 apply (s. 18); return of property (s. 19).
5. **Ta'zir haraabah (s. 20)** — where hadd is not attracted or not proved, the **PPC** punishment for **dacoity, robbery or extortion**.

## 4.12 Other provisions

1. **Rassagiri or patharidari (s. 21)** — extending **patronage, protection or harbour** to **cattle thieves** in return for a share — RI up to **14 years**, or whipping up to 70 stripes, **confiscation** of all immovable property and fine.
2. **Attempt (s. 22)** — imprisonment up to **10 years**.
3. **PPC provisions applied (s. 23)** — ss. **34–38** (joint liability), **71–72** and **149**; **abetment** of a hadd offence is punished as **ta'zir**.
4. **CrPC (s. 24)** — applies; if the evidence shows a **different offence** under another law, the court may convict of it; trial by the **Court of Session**; **appeal to the Federal Shariat Court** for ss. 9 and 17 and for any sentence exceeding **two years**.

# Chapter 5 · The Prohibition (Enforcement of Hadd) Order 1979

## 5.1 Definitions — Art. 2

**Adult** — 18 or puberty; **intoxicant** — an article in the Schedule, including **intoxicating liquor** (toddy, spirits, beer and all liquids containing alcohol normally used for intoxication — not solid intoxicants even if liquefied); **public place** — a street, park, or a place to which the public has access, including a **hotel, restaurant, club**, but **not the residential room** of a hotel occupied by a person; **Prohibition Officer**, manufacture, sale (any transfer, including a gift).

## 5.2 Offences and punishments

{w:14,56,30}
|Art. 3(1)|**Import, export, transport, manufacture, process, bottle, sell or serve** any intoxicant, or allow it on premises|Up to **5 years**, **30 stripes**, and fine|
|Art. 3(2)|The same, or **financing**, for **opium, coca leaf** or their derivatives|**Life**, or not less than **2 years**, 30 stripes, fine|
|Art. 4|**Owning, possessing or keeping** any intoxicant — not for a **non-Muslim** keeping a reasonable quantity for a **religious ceremony**|Up to **2 years**, or 30 stripes, and fine; **heroin or cocaine over 10 g**, opium or coca leaf over 1 kg — life or not less than 2 years|
|Art. 8|**Drinking liable to hadd** — an **adult Muslim** taking **intoxicating liquor by mouth**|**80 stripes** (after confirmation by the appellate court)|
|Art. 11|**Drinking liable to ta'zir** — (a) a Muslim whose drinking is not liable to hadd or not proved by Art. 9 evidence; (b) a **non-Muslim citizen** drinking except as part of a religious ceremony; (c) a **non-Muslim foreigner** drinking at a **public place**|Up to **3 years**, or 30 stripes, or both|
|Art. 12(2)|Police officer **detaining or arresting** on suspicion of drinking **without** first offering a **medical examination**|Up to 6 months or fine Rs. 500|
|Art. 20|Breach of **licence** conditions|Life or not less than 2 years and fine|

## 5.3 Drinking, proof and arrest

1. **Drinking (Art. 6)** — **intentionally**, and **without ikrah or iztirar**, taking an intoxicant by any means, **whether or not it intoxicates**. **Ikrah** — fear of injury to person, property or honour; **iztirar** — apprehension of death from extreme **hunger, thirst or serious illness**.
2. **Two kinds (Art. 7)** — liable to **hadd** or to **ta'zir**.
3. **Proof of hadd (Art. 9)** — **confession** before a competent court, **or** at least **two Muslim adult male witnesses** of integrity (tazkiyah-al-shuhood).
4. **Hadd not enforced (Art. 10)** — on **retraction**, or a witness resiling so that fewer than two remain; the court may order a **retrial**.
5. **Arrest (Art. 12)** — no police officer shall detain or arrest a person on suspicion of drinking unless he has asked him to accompany him to an **authorised medical officer** and he **refuses**, or is **certified** to have taken an intoxicant.
6. **Confiscation (Arts. 14–15)** — the intoxicant, apparatus, receptacles, animals and **vehicles** used; even on **acquittal**; where the offender is unknown, by the **Collector** after 15 days' notice.
7. **Cognizance (Art. 16)** — Art. 3 offences, and Arts. 4, 8, 11 at a **public place**, are **cognizable**.
8. **Licences (Art. 17)** — for bona fide **medicinal, scientific, industrial** purposes, for **non-Muslims' religious ceremonies** and **foreigners**, and for **export**.
9. **Repeat offence (Art. 24)**, **attempt (Art. 25)** — half the longest term (two years RI for Art. 8); **PPC ss. 34–38, 63–72 and Chapters V and V-A** apply (Art. 26).
10. **Narcotics** are now chiefly dealt with under the **Control of Narcotic Substances Act 1997**.

# Chapter 6 · The Execution of the Punishment of Whipping Ordinance 1979

1. **Application (s. 1(3))** — to whipping imposed under **any law** (today, the Hudood laws); it **overrides** other laws (s. 3).
2. **The whip (s. 4)** — a **single piece**, preferably of **leather**, a **cane** or a **branch of a tree**, with **no knob or joint**, not exceeding **1.22 metres** in length and **1.25 cm** in thickness (excluding the handle).
**Conditions and mode of execution (s. 5):**

{w:10,90}
|Cl.|Condition|
|(a)|**Medical examination** first, to ensure the punishment will **not cause death**|
|(b)|If the convict is **too old or weak**, the stripes are applied in a manner and at **intervals** that do not cause death|
|(c)|An **ill** convict — **postponed** until certified fit|
|(d)|A **pregnant woman** — postponed until **two months after the birth** of the child or miscarriage|
|(e)|Weather **too hot or too cold** — postponed until normal|
|(f)|Executed in the **presence of the authorised medical officer** at a **public place** appointed by the Provincial Government|
|(g)|The executioner must be **impartial** and of **mature understanding**|
|(h)|**moderate force**, **without raising his hand above his head**, so as **not to lacerate the skin**|
|(i)|After each stripe, the whip is **raised aloft**, not pulled off|
|(j)|Stripes **spread over the body**, **not on the head, face, stomach, chest or delicate parts**|
|(k)|The convict keeps the **clothes required by the Injunctions of Islam**|
|(l)|A **male standing**, a **female sitting**|
|(m)|If, during execution, the medical officer apprehends **death**, the rest is **postponed**|

**Custody (s. 6)** — a convict sentenced only to whipping is treated as in **simple imprisonment** until execution; if he cannot undergo whipping (old age, ill-health), the case is referred to the **court**, which may order execution in such manner as it thinks fit.

## 7.4 The 25 points of Topic 4

:::recap Revision sheet
1. Hadd — fixed by Quran / Sunnah; ta'zir — any other punishment; doubt averts hadd.
2. Tazkiyah-al-shuhood — court's inquiry into witnesses' truthfulness and abstention from major sins.
3. Five laws of 10 February 1979 — Ordinances VI (property), VII (zina), VIII (qazf), IX (whipping), P.O. 4 (prohibition).
4. 2006 Act — rape to PPC; fornication s. 496B; complaint-only (CrPC ss. 203A, 203C); no conversion (s. 5A); s. 156B; qazf on failed complaint.
5. Adult — male 18, female 16 or puberty (Zina Ord.); 18 or puberty (Property Ord., Prohibition Order).
6. Muhsan (zina) — Muslim, adult, sane, has had intercourse within a valid marriage.
7. Zina (s. 4); hadd zina (s. 5) — stoning if muhsan, 100 stripes otherwise; FSC confirmation.
8. Proof (s. 8) — confession before the Sessions Court, or four Muslim adult male eye-witnesses of penetration.
9. s. 9 — retraction; witnesses below four; s. 17 — stoning by witnesses; may be shot.
10. Sessions Court at tehsil headquarters; appeal to FSC; Muslim presiding officer.
11. Qazf (s. 3) — imputation of zina to harm reputation or hurt feelings; deceased; ironical; two exceptions.
12. Hadd qazf (s. 5) — adult, intentional, unambiguous, against a muhsan capable of intercourse; calling a person illegitimate = qazf of his mother.
13. Proof (s. 6) — confession; in court's presence; two witnesses; s. 6(2) — failed zina complaint.
14. 80 stripes + evidence inadmissible (s. 7); complaint by the victim or his ascendants / descendants (s. 8); not against descendants; truth (s. 9).
15. Lian (s. 14) — four oaths and a curse each; dissolution, no appeal; DMMA s. 2(viia).
16. Hirz — arrangement for custody; house (door open or shut), almirah, box, container, custody of a person (paid or not); one family = one hirz.
17. Hadd theft (s. 5) — adult, surreptitious (day / night rule), from hirz, nisab, not stolen property, knowledge of value.
18. Nisab — 4.457 g of gold at the time of theft; no aggregation across transactions or hirz.
19. Proof (s. 7) — plea of guilty, or two Muslim adult male eye-witnesses other than the victim; victim's statement first.
20. Punishment (s. 9) — right hand at wrist; left foot at ankle; life (till death); FSC confirmation; medical officer; postponement if death feared; no Chapter XXIX remission.

22. Not enforced (s. 11) — retraction, witnesses below two, victim withdraws, limbs missing; ta'zir under PPC (ss. 13–14).
23. Haraabah (ss. 15–20) — show of force to take property; 30 stripes + 3 years RI; hand and foot; death if murder; ta'zir — PPC dacoity / robbery / extortion.
24. Prohibition — Art. 3 (5 years + 30 stripes), Art. 4 (possession), Art. 8 (adult Muslim by mouth — 80 stripes), Art. 9 (confession or two witnesses), Art. 11 (ta'zir — 3 years / 30 stripes), Art. 12 (medical examination before arrest).
25. Whipping — whip ≤ 1.22 m × 1.25 cm; medical examination; pregnant — two months after birth; not on head, face, stomach, chest; male standing, female sitting.
