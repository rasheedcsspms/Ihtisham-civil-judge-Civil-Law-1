# How to use this knowledge base

## What Topic 4 covers

1. **Foundations** — hadd and ta'zir, the Islamic standard of proof, the 1979 laws and the **2006** reforms, and the **Federal Shariat Court's** appellate jurisdiction.
2. The **Zina** Ordinance — zina, zina liable to hadd, **muhsan**, proof by **confession or four witnesses**, cases where hadd is not enforced, and the complaint procedure (CrPC s. 203A).
3. The **Qazf** Ordinance — qazf, qazf liable to hadd, proof, punishment (80 stripes), who may complain, and **lian**.
4. The **Offences against Property** Ordinance — **theft liable to hadd**, **hirz**, **nisab**, proof, punishment and its **implementation**, exemptions, **haraabah**, rassagiri (KPHC 2026).
5. The **Prohibition** Order — intoxicants, **drinking liable to hadd and to ta'zir**, proof, arrest, confiscation.
6. The **Whipping** Ordinance — the whip and the mode of execution.

:::ict Why the Hudood laws apply in the ICT
All five laws "extend to the whole of Pakistan" (s. 1 of each) and were applied to the federal capital from their commencement on **12 Rabi-ul-Awwal 1399 AH (10 February 1979)**. Trials of hadd offences are held by the **Court of Session**, not by a s. 30 Magistrate, ordinarily at the **tehsil headquarters**; appeals lie to the **Federal Shariat Court** (Art. 203DD of the Constitution and the proviso to the relevant section of each Ordinance). The **presiding officer** must be a **Muslim** (unless the accused is non-Muslim).
:::
