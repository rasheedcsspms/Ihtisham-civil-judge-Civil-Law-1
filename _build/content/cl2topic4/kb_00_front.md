# How to use this knowledge base

## What Topic 4 covers

**Part A — the Specific Relief Act 1877**

1. The nature of specific relief (ss. 5–7).
2. Recovery of **possession** — ss. 8, 9 (summary suit) and ss. 10, 11 (movables).
3. **Specific performance** — when it is granted (s. 12), partial performance (ss. 13–17), contracts that cannot be enforced (s. 21), **discretion** (s. 22), **who may obtain** it (s. 23) and **personal bars** (ss. 24–25), **against whom** it may be enforced (s. 27, 27A) and **who cannot be compelled** (s. 28), and the effect of dismissal (s. 29).
4. **Rectification** (ss. 31–34), **rescission** (ss. 35–38) and **cancellation** (ss. 39–41) — distinguished.
5. **Declaratory decrees** (ss. 42–43).
6. **Injunctions** — perpetual and mandatory (ss. 52–57).

**Part B — the Registration Act 1908**

7. Documents of which registration is **compulsory** (s. 17, with the **2022** additions) and **optional** (s. 18).
8. **Time** and **place** for presentation (ss. 23–31).
9. **Who may present** a document; **powers of attorney** (ss. 32–33).
10. **Enquiry** and **admission or denial** of execution (ss. 34–35).
11. **Refusal** to register — grounds, and the **remedies** (ss. 71–77).
12. **Effects** of registration and non-registration (ss. 47–50).

:::ict Pakistani features
1. **SRA s. 42(2)** (Act XXXIII of 2023): a declaratory suit "shall be **decided by the Court within six months** and the appellate court shall decide the appeal **not later than ninety days**".
2. **Registration Act s. 17(1)(ba)** (Registration (Amendment) Act 2022): **compulsory registration** of an instrument that **grants power to an attorney** in respect of immovable property, or that constitutes an **agreement to sell** immovable property, of the value of Rs. 100 or more.
3. **Registration Act s. 49** (Pakistani text) has **no proviso** of the Indian kind; instead **s. 50** has two provisos — protection of **part performance** under **s. 53A of the Transfer of Property Act**, and the right of a holder of an unregistered document to sue for **specific performance** against a later registered transferee, subject to **s. 27(b) SRA**.
4. **SRA s. 9** — no suit against the **Federal or a Provincial Government**; **no appeal or review**; limitation **six months** (Limitation Act, Art. 3).
5. **SRA Chapter VIII** (public duties) was omitted in 1981; writs lie under **Art. 199** of the Constitution.
:::
