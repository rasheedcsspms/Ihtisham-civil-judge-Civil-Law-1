
# Chapter 1 · Qisas and Diyat — the framework

## 1.1 Background

Until 1990 Pakistan's law of homicide and hurt was Macaulay's (murder, culpable homicide, grievous hurt). The **Federal Shariat Court** (*Muhammad Riaz v. Federal Government*, PLD 1980 FSC 1) and then the **Shariat Appellate Bench of the Supreme Court** (*Federation of Pakistan v. Gul Hassan Khan*, PLD 1989 SC 633) held those provisions **repugnant to the Injunctions of Islam** because they ignored **qisas**, **diyat** and the right of the victim's heirs to **forgive or compound**. The **Qisas and Diyat Ordinance 1990** (re-promulgated many times) replaced them, and was finally enacted as the **Criminal Law (Amendment) Act 1997 (Act II of 1997)**, which substituted **ss. 299 to 338H**. The court is to be **guided by the Injunctions of Islam** in interpreting the Chapter (s. 338F).

## 1.2 Definitions — s. 299

|Term|Meaning|
|**Adult**|A person who has attained **eighteen** years (s. 299(a)); "minor" — not an adult (s. 299(i))|
|**Arsh**|Compensation **specified** in the Chapter, payable to the victim or his heirs (s. 299(b))|
|**Daman**|Compensation **determined by the court**, payable to the victim, for hurt **not liable to arsh** (s. 299(d))|
|**Diyat**|Compensation specified in **s. 323**, payable to the **heirs** of the victim (s. 299(e))|
|**Fasad-fil-arz**|Includes the **past conduct** of the offender, a **previous conviction**, the **brutal or shocking manner** of the offence **outrageous to the public conscience**, the offender being a **potential danger to the community**, or an offence committed **in the name or on the pretext of honour** (s. 299(ee), 2016)|
|**Ikrah-e-tam**|Putting a person, his spouse or a blood relation within the prohibited degrees in fear of **instant death**, **instant permanent impairing of an organ**, or **instant fear of sodomy or zina-bil-jabr** (s. 299(g))|
|**Ikrah-e-naqis**|Any duress **not amounting** to ikrah-e-tam (s. 299(h))|

|**Qatl**|Causing **death** of a person (s. 299(j))|
|**Qisas**|Punishment by causing **similar hurt** at the **same part of the body** of the convict as he caused to the victim, or by causing his **death** if he committed **qatl-i-amd**, in exercise of the right of the victim or a **wali** (s. 299(k))|

# Chapter 2 · Qatl — the four kinds

![The four kinds of qatl](qatl_kinds.png)

## 2.1 Qatl-i-amd — s. 300

> "300. Whoever, with the **intention of causing death** or with the **intention of causing bodily injury** to a person, by doing an act which **in the ordinary course of nature is likely to cause death**, or with the **knowledge that his act is so imminently dangerous that it must in all probability cause death**, causes the death of such person, is said to commit qatl-e-amd."

**Three limbs:** (1) intention to cause **death**; (2) intention to cause **bodily injury** by an act likely in the ordinary course of nature to cause death; (3) **knowledge** that the act is so imminently dangerous that it must in all probability cause death (e.g., firing into a crowd).

**Transferred malice — s. 301:** where a person, by doing anything which he intends or knows to be likely to cause death, causes the death of a person whose death he neither intends nor knows himself likely to cause, he is liable for **qatl-i-amd**.

## 2.2 Punishment of qatl-i-amd — s. 302

Whoever commits qatl-i-amd shall, subject to the Chapter, be —

1. **(a)** punished with **death as qisas**; or
2. **(b)** punished with **death or imprisonment for life as ta'zir**, having regard to the facts and circumstances of the case, **if the proof in either of the forms specified in s. 304 is not available**; or
3. **(c)** punished with imprisonment of either description up to **twenty-five years**, where **according to the Injunctions of Islam the punishment of qisas is not applicable** —
4. **Proviso (2016):** clause (c) shall **not** apply where the principle of **fasad-fil-arz** is attracted; then only (a) or (b) applies.

**When does s. 302(c) apply?** The courts have applied it where qisas is not applicable under Islamic law although the killing is intentional — e.g., the accused **exceeded the right of private defence**, acted on **grave and sudden provocation**, in a **sudden fight**, or other situations equivalent to the old exceptions to murder. (The old Exceptions to s. 300 no longer exist; their substance now operates through s. 302(c) and through mitigation.)

## 2.3 Proof of qatl-i-amd liable to qisas — s. 304

Qisas (death as qisas) requires one of two **forms of proof**:

1. the accused makes before a **court competent to try** the offence a **voluntary and true confession**; or
2. evidence as provided in **Article 17 of the Qanun-e-Shahadat 1984** (the Islamic standard of witnesses — two adult Muslim male witnesses of good character, or as the Injunctions of Islam require).

If neither form is available, the court may still convict on other evidence and award **death or life as ta'zir** (s. 302(b)). In practice almost all death sentences are **ta'zir**, not qisas. The same proof rule applies to **hurt liable to qisas** (s. 304(2)).

## 2.4 Wali — s. 305

In case of qatl, the **wali** is (a) the **heirs of the victim** according to his **personal law** — **but not** the accused or the convict where qatl-i-amd is committed in the name or on the pretext of **honour** (2016); and (b) the **Government**, if there is **no heir**.

## 2.5 Qatl-i-amd not liable to qisas — s. 306

1. **(a)** the offender is a **minor or insane** — *proviso:* a person liable to qisas who associates himself with a person not liable, to save himself, is **not** exempted;
2. **(b)** the offender causes the death of his **child or grandchild**, how low-so-ever;
3. **(c)** any **wali** of the victim is a **direct descendant**, how low-so-ever, of the offender (e.g., a husband kills his wife and their son is her heir — the son cannot claim qisas against his father).

## 2.6 Qisas not enforced — s. 307

1. **(a)** the offender **dies** before enforcement;
2. **(b)** any wali **voluntarily and without duress**, to the satisfaction of the court, **waives** (s. 309) or **compounds** (s. 310);
3. **(c)** the right of qisas **devolves on the offender** (on the death of the wali), or on a person who has **no right of qisas** against him (*illus.*: A kills his son's maternal uncle Z; the right passes to A's wife, then on her death to their son B, who cannot claim qisas against his father).

The court must **record the statement of the wali on oath** and an **opinion** that the waiver or composition is **voluntary** and **not the result of duress** (s. 307(2)).

## 2.7 Punishment where qisas is not available — s. 308

Where the offender is **not liable** to qisas (s. 306) or qisas is not enforceable under **s. 307(c)**, he is liable to **diyat** — from the property of a minor or insane offender or by such person as the court determines. **Plus ta'zir:** a minor of sufficient maturity, or an insane person in a lucid interval, may also get imprisonment up to **25 years**; where qisas is unenforceable under s. 307(c) and there is **no wali other than the offender**, imprisonment up to 25 years as ta'zir; and in any case the court may add imprisonment up to **25 years** as ta'zir (s. 308(2)).

## 2.8 Waiver (afw) — s. 309

1. An **adult sane wali** may **at any time** and **without compensation** **waive** his right of qisas.
2. **Not** where the **Government** is the wali, or the right vests in a **minor or insane** person.
3. Where there are several walis, **any one** may waive; the others are entitled to their **share of diyat**.
4. Waiver by the wali of **one victim** does not affect the right of the wali of **another victim**; waiver against **one offender** does not affect the right against **another**.
5. **Proviso (2016):** where **fasad-fil-arz** is attracted, waiver is **subject to s. 311**.

## 2.9 Compounding (sulh) — s. 310

1. An adult sane wali may **at any time**, on accepting **badal-i-sulh** (mutually agreed compensation according to Shari'ah — cash, kind, movable or immovable property), **compound** the right of qisas.
2. **Proviso:** a **female shall not be given in marriage or otherwise** in badal-i-sulh (2005); **s. 310A** punishes giving a female in marriage as **badla-e-sulh, wanni or swara** with **3 to 7 years** and fine of **Rs. 500,000**.
3. A **minor or insane wali** — his own wali may compound for him, but badal-i-sulh must **not be less than diyat**; same where the **Government** is the wali.
4. Where badal-i-sulh is **not determined** or cannot be valued in money, qisas is deemed compounded and the offender is liable to **diyat**.
5. Badal-i-sulh may be paid on demand or on a **deferred date** as agreed.
6. **Proviso (2016):** where fasad-fil-arz is attracted, compounding is subject to **s. 311**.

## 2.10 Ta'zir after waiver or compounding — s. 311

> "311. Where **all the wali do not waive or compound** the right of qisas, or if the principle of **fasad-fil-arz** is attracted, the court may, having regard to the facts and circumstances of the case, punish an offender against whom the right of qisas has been waived or compounded with **death or imprisonment for life or imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to fourteen years** as ta'zir:
> Provided that if the offence has been committed **in the name or on the pretext of honour**, the punishment shall be **imprisonment for life**."

**Effect:** in an honour killing, a compromise with the family (often the killer's own relatives) can no longer secure release — the court **must** award at least **life imprisonment**.

## 2.11 Other qatl-i-amd rules — ss. 312 to 314, 317

1. **s. 312** — a wali who kills a convict after waiver or compounding: **qisas** if he waived or knew of it; **diyat** if he did not know.
2. **s. 313** — the right of qisas vests in **each** wali; if there is none, in the **Government**; for a minor or insane wali, in his **father or paternal grandfather**, otherwise the Government.
3. **s. 314** — qisas executed by a **functionary of Government** by causing death as the court directs; **all walis** must be present (in person or by written representatives) — if a wali fails to attend after notice, an officer authorised by the court permits execution; a **pregnant woman** — execution postponed up to **two years after the birth** of the child, bail on security or treatment as simple imprisonment.
4. **s. 317** — a person committing **qatl-i-amd or qatl shibh-i-amd** is **debarred from succeeding** to the victim's estate as heir or legatee.
5. **s. 338D** — a sentence of death (qisas or ta'zir) or of qisas for hurt is **not executed unless confirmed by the High Court** (also CrPC s. 374).

## 2.12 Qatl shibh-i-amd — ss. 315, 316

"Whoever, with **intent to cause harm** to the body or mind of any person, causes the death of that or of any other person by means of a **weapon or an act which in the ordinary course of nature is not likely to cause death**." *Illus.:* A strikes Z with a **stick or stone** not likely to cause death; Z dies. **Punishment:** **diyat**, and may also be imprisonment up to **25 years** as ta'zir.

## 2.13 Qatl-i-khata — ss. 318 to 320

"Whoever, **without any intention** to cause the death of, or cause harm to, a person causes death of such person, either by **mistake of act** or by **mistake of fact**." *Illus. (a):* A aims at a deer, misses and kills Z standing by (mistake of act); *(b):* A shoots at what he believes is a boar but it is a man (mistake of fact). **Punishment (s. 319):** **diyat**; if by a **rash or negligent act** (other than driving), also up to **5 years** as ta'zir. **Rash or negligent driving (s. 320):** diyat **and** imprisonment up to **10 years**.

## 2.14 Qatl-bis-sabab — ss. 321, 322

"Whoever, without any intention to cause death of, or cause harm to, any person, does any **unlawful act** which **becomes a cause** for the death of another person." *Illus.:* A unlawfully digs a pit in a thoroughfare; B falls in and dies. **Punishment:** **diyat** only.

|Kind|Mental element|Example|Punishment|
|Qatl-i-amd (s. 300)|Intention to kill / injure fatally; or knowledge of imminent danger|Shooting at the chest|Death (qisas); death or life (ta'zir); up to 25 years (s. 302(c))|
|Qatl shibh-i-amd (s. 315)|Intent to **harm** with a means **not likely** to kill|Blow with a light stick|Diyat + up to 25 years|
|Qatl-i-khata (s. 318)|**No intention** — mistake of act or fact|Hunting accident|Diyat (+ 5 years if rash; + 10 years if driving)|
|Qatl-bis-sabab (s. 321)|No intention — an **unlawful act** becomes the cause|Unlawful pit in a road|Diyat only|

## 2.15 Attempt to commit qatl-i-amd — s. 324

Whoever does any act with such intention or knowledge, and in such circumstances, that if he caused qatl he would be guilty of qatl-i-amd: imprisonment up to **10 years** (not less than **5 years** if in the name of **honour**) and **fine**; and if hurt is caused, **in addition** the punishment for the hurt. If the punishment for the hurt is qisas which is not executable — **arsh** plus up to **7 years**. The offence is **compoundable** by the person against whom the act was committed (CrPC s. 345(2)).

## 2.16 Other offences in Chapter XVI

1. **Thug** (ss. 326–327) — habitually associated with others for robbery or child-stealing by qatl — imprisonment for life.
2. **Exposure and abandonment** of a child under **12** (s. 328) — up to 7 years.
3. **Cruelty to a child** (s. 328A, 2016) — assault, ill-treatment, neglect causing physical or psychological injury — 1 to 3 years, or fine Rs. 25,000–50,000.
4. **Concealment of birth** by secret disposal of the dead body (s. 329) — up to 2 years.

## 2.17 Waiver and compounding of all Chapter XVI offences — s. 338E

1. **All** offences under the Chapter may be **waived or compounded**, subject to the Chapter and **CrPC s. 345**; ss. 309, 310 and 311 apply mutatis mutandis.
2. Where an offence has been waived or compounded, the court may, in its discretion, **acquit** or award **ta'zir** according to the nature of the offence.
3. **Proviso (2016):** where **fasad-fil-arz** is attracted, the court **shall** punish the offender with imprisonment or fine as provided for the offence.
4. Questions of waiver or compounding are decided by the **trial court**, or by the **appellate court** during the pendency of an appeal (s. 338E(2)).

*Who may compound (CrPC s. 345(2) table):* qatl-i-amd (s. 302), qatl under ikrah (s. 303), s. 308 — **by the heirs of the victim** (subject to s. 311); s. 316, 319, 320, 322 — by the heirs; **s. 324** — by the **person against whom the act was committed**; all hurts under ss. 334–337L — by the **person to whom hurt is caused**; ss. 338A, 338C — the victim or heirs. The **acid-attack** offence (s. 336B) is **not** in the list.

# Chapter 3 · Hurt (ss. 332 to 337Z)

## 3.1 Hurt and its kinds — s. 332

"Whoever causes **pain, harm, disease, infirmity or injury** to any person, or impairs, disables, **disfigures, defaces** or dismembers any organ of the body or part thereof of any person **without causing his death**, is said to cause hurt." **Disfigure** (Explanation, 2011) — disfigurement of the face, or of any organ, which impairs, injures, corrodes or deforms the symmetry or appearance of a person.

![The kinds of hurt](hurt_kinds.png)

**Kinds (s. 332(2)):** (a) **itlaf-i-udw**; (b) **itlaf-i-salahiyyat-i-udw**; (c) **shajjah**; (d) **jurh**; and (e) **all kinds of other hurts**.

## 3.2 Itlaf-i-udw and itlaf-i-salahiyyat-i-udw — ss. 333 to 336

1. **Itlaf-i-udw** (s. 333) — **dismembering, amputating, severing** any limb or organ.
2. **Itlaf-i-salahiyyat-i-udw** (s. 335) — **destroying or permanently impairing the functioning, power or capacity** of an organ, or causing **permanent disfigurement** (e.g., blinding an eye while it remains).
3. **Punishment (ss. 334, 336):** **qisas**, in consultation with the **authorised medical officer**; if qisas is **not executable** keeping in view the **principles of equality** under the Injunctions of Islam — **arsh** and imprisonment up to **10 years** as ta'zir.

## 3.3 Hurt by corrosive substance — ss. 336A, 336B (2011)

Hurt caused intentionally or knowingly by a **corrosive substance** — acid, poison, explosive, heating substance, noxious thing, arsenic or any chemical with a corroding effect that is deleterious to the human body — is punished with **imprisonment for life** or not less than **14 years**, and a **minimum fine of Rs. 1 million**. The offence is **not compoundable**.

## 3.4 Shajjah — ss. 337, 337A (hurt on the head or face)

"Whoever causes, on the **head or face** of any person, any hurt which does not amount to itlaf-i-udw or itlaf-i-salahiyyat-i-udw."

## 3.5 Jurh — ss. 337B to 337F (hurt on the body other than head or face)

"Whoever causes on any part of the body **other than the head or face** a hurt which **leaves a mark of the wound**, whether temporary or permanent." **Two kinds:** **jaifah** and **ghayr-jaifah**.

1. **Jaifah** (s. 337C) — the injury **extends to the body cavity of the trunk** (chest or abdomen). **Punishment (s. 337D):** arsh **one-third** of diyat + up to **10 years**.
2. **Ghayr-jaifah** (s. 337E) — jurh not amounting to jaifah. **Six kinds (LHC 2023):**

|**Mutalahimah**|**Lacerating** the flesh|Daman + up to 3 years|

## 3.6 Other hurts — ss. 337G to 337L

1. **s. 337G** — hurt by **rash or negligent driving**: arsh or daman + up to **5 years**.
2. **s. 337H** — hurt by a **rash or negligent act** (other than driving): arsh or daman + up to **3 years**; and **endangering** life or safety by a rash act — up to 3 months or fine.
3. **s. 337I** — hurt by **mistake (khata)**: arsh or daman only.
4. **s. 337J** — hurt by **poison** or stupefying drug with intent to hurt or to commit an offence: + up to **10 years**.
5. **s. 337K** — hurt to **extort a confession** or information, or to compel restoration of property: + up to **10 years** (a typical police-torture offence).
6. **s. 337L** — **other hurt**: (1) which **endangers life** or causes **severe bodily pain for 20 days** or more or inability to follow **ordinary pursuits for 20 days** — daman + up to **7 years**; (2) any other hurt — up to **2 years**, or daman, or both.

## 3.7 Hurt not liable to qisas — s. 337M (ASJ 2025)

1. **(a)** the offender is a **minor or insane** — liable to **arsh** and **ta'zir** (having regard to age, circumstances and nature of hurt);
2. **(b)** the offender causes hurt to the victim **at the victim's instance** (consent) — may be liable to ta'zir;
3. **(c)** the offender caused itlaf-i-udw of a **physically imperfect organ** of the victim and the convict **does not suffer** from a similar imperfection — arsh (+ ta'zir); *illus.*: half of Z's right ear already missing; A's ear is perfect — arsh, not qisas; but if Z's ear was physically perfect though **deaf**, or merely **pierced**, A is liable to qisas;
4. **(d)** the **organ of the offender** liable to qisas is **missing** — arsh (+ ta'zir).

## 3.8 Qisas for hurt not enforced — s. 337N (ASJ 2025)

1. **(a)** the offender **dies** before execution;
2. **(b)** the offender's **organ is lost** before execution — arsh (+ ta'zir);
3. **(c)** the victim **waives** qisas or **compounds** with badal-i-sulh;
4. **(d)** the right of qisas **devolves on a person who cannot claim qisas** against the offender — arsh if there is a wali other than the offender, otherwise ta'zir.
5. **s. 337N(2)** — in **all** cases of hurt the court may, in addition to arsh, award **ta'zir** to a **previous convict, habitual or hardened, desperate or dangerous criminal**, or where the offence is in the name of **honour** — not less than **one-third** of the maximum imprisonment for the hurt.

## 3.9 Wali in case of hurt — s. 337O (LHC 2023; ASJ 2025)

1. **(a)** the **victim** himself — if he is a **minor or insane**, his right of qisas is exercised by his **father or paternal grandfather**, how high-so-ever;
2. **(b)** the **heirs** of the victim, if he dies before the execution of qisas;
3. **(c)** the **Government**, in the absence of the victim or his heirs.

## 3.10 Execution of qisas for hurt — s. 337P (ASJ 2025)

1. Executed **in public** by an **authorised medical officer**, who must first **examine the offender** and take due care that the execution does **not cause death** or **exceed the hurt** caused to the victim.
2. The **wali shall be present**; if he or his representative is absent after notice, an officer authorised by the court permits execution.
3. A **pregnant woman** — execution may be postponed up to **two years after the birth** of the child, with bail on security or treatment as simple imprisonment.
4. Confirmation by the **High Court** first (s. 338D).

## 3.11 Arsh — ss. 337Q to 337X

|Organ|Arsh|
|**Single organs** (s. 337Q) — nose, tongue|**Full diyat**|
|**Organs in pairs** (s. 337R) — hands, feet, eyes, lips, breasts|**Both: full diyat; one: one-half of diyat**; but if the victim had **only one** such organ (the other missing or incapacitated), itlaf of the remaining one — **full diyat** (LHC 2023)|
|**Organs in fours** (s. 337S) — eyelids|One — **¼**; two — **½**; three — **¾**; all four — **full diyat**|
|**Fingers** (s. 337T)|A finger of hand or foot — **1/10** of diyat; a joint — the official text prints **one-thirteenth** (commonly read as a misprint for one-thirtieth, i.e., a third of a finger); a joint of the **thumb** — **1/20**|
|**Teeth** (s. 337U)|A tooth (not milk tooth) — **1/20**; **twenty or more** teeth — **full diyat**; a milk tooth — daman + up to 1 year (arsh if it impedes the new tooth)|
|**Hair** (s. 337V)|All hair of head, beard, moustache, eyebrows, eyelashes etc. uprooted — **full diyat** + up to 3 years; one eyebrow — **½**; one eyelash — **¼**; forcible removal by other process — daman + up to 1 year|


**Merger — s. 337W:** separate arsh for each hurt, **but** hurt to an organ and to a part of the same organ — arsh for the **organ** only (*illus.*: fingers then the hand — arsh for the hand); wounds that **join** into one — arsh for one wound; and where the offender, after causing hurt, causes death by qatl liable to diyat **before the wound heals**, the arsh **merges** into the diyat.

**Payment — s. 337X:** lump sum or instalments over **five years**; on default, jail as simple imprisonment, or bail on security or surety, or parole; recovery from the estate after death. **Disbursement — s. 337Z:** to the victim, or his heirs by their shares.

## 3.12 Miscarriage — ss. 338 to 338C

1. **Isqat-i-haml** (s. 338) — causing a woman with child **whose organs have not been formed** to miscarry, except in good faith to save her life or for necessary treatment; the woman herself is within the section. **Punishment (s. 338A):** up to **3 years** with her consent, up to **10 years** without; plus punishment for hurt or death caused.
2. **Isqat-i-janin** (s. 338B) — miscarriage of a child **some of whose limbs or organs have been formed**. **Punishment (s. 338C):** **1/20 of diyat** if born dead; **full diyat** if born alive and dies; up to **7 years** as ta'zir; separate diyat for each child.

:::recap Qisas and Diyat in ten lines
- Background: *Gul Hassan* (PLD 1989 SC 633) → Ordinance 1990 → Act II of 1997; guided by Quran and Sunnah (s. 338F).
- Qatl: amd (s. 300) / shibh-i-amd (s. 315) / khata (s. 318) / bis-sabab (s. 321).
- s. 302: (a) qisas — needs s. 304 proof (confession before court, or Art. 17 QSO witnesses); (b) death or life as ta'zir; (c) up to 25 years where qisas not applicable — not where fasad-fil-arz.
- Wali — heirs (not the honour-killer), else Government (s. 305); no qisas — minor / insane, own child, descendant wali (s. 306); not enforced — death, waiver / sulh, devolution (s. 307).
- Afw (s. 309) any adult sane wali, without compensation; sulh (s. 310) with badal-i-sulh, never a woman (s. 310A: 3–7 years); s. 311 ta'zir up to death; honour → life.
- Diyat ≥ 30,630 g silver (s. 323), 5-year instalments (s. 331); s. 317 killer cannot inherit.
- Hurt: itlaf-i-udw / salahiyyat (qisas or arsh + 10 years), acid (life or 14+ and Rs. 1 m), shajjah (head / face, 6 kinds), jurh (jaifah ⅓ diyat; ghayr-jaifah — daman, 6 kinds), other hurt (20-day rule).
- No qisas for hurt (s. 337M) — minor / insane, consent, imperfect organ, offender's organ missing; not enforced (s. 337N) — death, organ lost, waiver, devolution.
- Wali in hurt — victim (father / grandfather for a minor), heirs, Government (s. 337O); execution in public by medical officer, wali present, pregnancy postponement (s. 337P); High Court confirmation (s. 338D).
- Arsh — single organ full; pairs ½ each; eyelids ¼ each; finger 1/10; tooth 1/20 (20 teeth full); hair; merger (s. 337W).
:::
