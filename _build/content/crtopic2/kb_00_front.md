# How to use this knowledge base

## What Topic 2 covers

1. **Qisas and Diyat** — the Islamic law of homicide and hurt that replaced the old ss. 299–338: definitions, the four kinds of **qatl**, **proof**, **wali**, **waiver (afw)** and **compounding (sulh)**, **ta'zir**, **fasad-fil-arz** and **honour** crimes, **diyat**; the kinds of **hurt** (itlaf-i-udw, itlaf-i-salahiyyat-i-udw, shajjah, jurh), **arsh** and **daman**, qisas for hurt, miscarriage.
2. Other offences against the **body** — wrongful restraint and confinement, assault, criminal force, outraging modesty.
3. **Rape**, gang rape, unnatural offences and **sexual abuse** of children.
4. **Kidnapping** and **abduction**.
5. **Offences against property** — theft, extortion, robbery, dacoity, misappropriation, criminal breach of trust, receiving stolen property, cheating, mischief, **criminal trespass** and house-breaking; **s. 489F**.
6. Offences against **women and marriage** — ss. 310A, 493A–498C, 509.
7. **Defamation** (ss. 499–502), **criminal intimidation**, and offences against **public tranquillity** and **public justice**.

:::ict Pakistani features
1. Chapter XVI ("Of offences affecting the human body") is **Islamic in content** — it uses qisas, diyat, arsh, daman and ta'zir, the right of the **wali**, and **compounding** by the heirs (s. 338E; CrPC s. 345(2)).
2. **Honour crimes (Act XLIII of 2016):** the accused cannot be a **wali** (s. 305(a)); waiver or compounding does not save him from ta'zir — the minimum is **imprisonment for life** (s. 311 proviso); **fasad-fil-arz** includes offences in the name of honour (s. 299(ee)).
3. **Rape (Act XLIV of 2016; Act LVI of 2021):** a wide, gender-neutral definition (s. 375); **gang rape** (s. 375A); punishment includes imprisonment for the **remainder of natural life** (s. 376).
4. **Criminal Laws (Amendment) Act 2025** removed the **death penalty** from s. 354A (stripping a woman) and s. 402C (harbouring hijackers).
5. **Attempt to commit suicide** (old s. 325) has been **omitted** (2022).
6. **Adultery** (old s. 497) was repealed in 1979; **fornication** is now s. 496B (Women's Protection Act 2006).
:::
