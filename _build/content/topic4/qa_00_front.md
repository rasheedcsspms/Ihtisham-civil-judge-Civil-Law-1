# How to use these model answers

## The questions

No past paper of the Islamabad High Court's civil judge examination is publicly available. The questions below are taken from the papers of courts that set the **same or a similar syllabus**. The **KP High Court 2026** paper follows the IHC syllabus most closely and devotes a whole part to the **Sale of Goods Act**. The Punjab University LL.B questions are the classic theory questions on this part of the syllabus.

## The five parts of every answer

1. **At a glance** — the source, the marks and the exact question.
2. **The answer map** — a one-page picture; each box is one numbered heading of the answer. Draw a rough version in the exam before writing.
3. **Decoding the question** — what the examiner wants.
4. **The model answer** — headings, bare-Act quotations, one case per point, an illustration and a conclusion.
5. **Why this answer scores** — the techniques to copy.

:::tip Pakistani law wins marks in this topic
Quote the Pakistani texts where they differ from Indian textbooks: **SGA s. 16A** (the seller's duty to disclose defects — 1994), the Pakistani **s. 13(2)** (only acceptance turns a condition into a warranty), **s. 37(2)** (rejection of excess goods that are hard to separate), **s. 2(7)** (electricity, water and gas are goods), **Contract Act ss. 178–178A** (pledges by mercantile agents and voidable-title holders), and **Art. 17 of the Qanun-e-Shahadat** (attestation of instruments of financial obligation, including guarantees).
:::
