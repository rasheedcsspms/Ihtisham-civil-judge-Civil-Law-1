# How to use this knowledge base

## What Topic 1 covers

1. The idea of a crime — **actus reus** and **mens rea**; strict liability; stages of a crime.
2. **Application** of the Code — territorial and extra-territorial (ss. 1–5).
3. **General explanations** — the definitions that run through the Code (ss. 6–52A), especially **good faith** and **harbour** (KPHC 2026).
4. **Joint liability** — common intention (s. 34), common object (s. 149), and ss. 35–38.
5. **Punishments** — the ten kinds (s. 53), commutation, fine, solitary confinement and **enhanced punishment for repeat offenders** (s. 75).
6. **General exceptions** (ss. 76–106) — mistake, judicial acts, accident, necessity, **infancy**, **insanity**, **intoxication**, consent, compulsion, trifles, and the **right of private defence**.
7. **Abetment** (ss. 107–120), **criminal conspiracy** (ss. 120A–120B) and **attempt** (s. 511).

:::ict Pakistani features you must not miss
1. **Age of criminal responsibility** raised in **2016**: nothing is an offence by a child **under ten** (s. 82); a child **above ten and under fourteen** is protected if of immature understanding (s. 83) (Act X of 2016). Older books still say seven and twelve.
2. **Punishments** start with **qisas, diyat, arsh, daman and ta'zir** (s. 53, as substituted by Act II of 1997).
3. **Imprisonment for life** = **twenty-five years** for calculating fractions (s. 57).
4. A death sentence for **qatl** cannot be **commuted** without the consent of the **heirs of the victim** (s. 54 proviso).
5. Section 100 (private defence extending to death) has **six** clauses in Pakistan — there is no acid-attack clause as in India.
6. **Juvenile Justice System Act 2018** governs the trial of persons under 18 (Topic 3).
:::
