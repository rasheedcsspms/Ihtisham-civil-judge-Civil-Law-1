# How to use this knowledge base

## The paper and what it asks

The General Knowledge paper tests "current world affairs and broad facts of historical, political, geographical and economic importance", with a section on **Islam and its system of administration of justice**, at **degree** level. Recent Civil Judge GK papers (Lahore High Court 2020, 2023) follow a pattern you should expect:

1. **Objective part** — 20 MCQs (Quran, Islamic history, Pakistan, world, science), short explanations and **abbreviations**.
2. **Essay part** — questions on Pakistan affairs (federalism, corruption, population, the Pakistan Movement).
3. **Islamic part** — e.g., "**Describe the Islamic concept of peace in the light of Quran and Sunnah**" (2023); "**the role of woman in Islam and modern society**" (2023); the Islamic **system of justice** (IHC syllabus); the KP High Court 2026 papers asked about **women as witnesses**, **tazkiyah al-shuhood**, **qisas, diyat, arsh, daman**, **ijtihad** and the **objects of punishment**.

## How this volume is organised

Each chapter gives the **facts** (for MCQs) and then the **concepts** (for essays). Chapter 12 has **model essay outlines**; Chapter 13 an **MCQ bank** of the most repeated facts.

:::tip Writing an Islamic Studies essay
1. **Begin** with a Quranic verse or hadith (with reference, e.g., "Surah an-Nisa 4:58") and a one-paragraph introduction defining the concept.
2. **Body** — 6 to 10 headings, each with a Quranic or prophetic proof and a historical example (the Prophet ﷺ, the Caliphs).
3. **Contrast** with the modern / Western position where relevant.
4. **Relevance to Pakistan** — the Constitution (Arts. 2, 2A, 31, 227), Federal Shariat Court, Council of Islamic Ideology.
5. **Conclude** with a balanced, forward-looking paragraph.
:::
