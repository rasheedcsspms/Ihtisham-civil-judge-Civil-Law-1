## 5.2 The war of 2026

1. **Start — 28 February 2026.** Joint US–Israeli strikes on Iran: US **Operation Epic Fury**, Israeli **Operation Roaring Lion**.
2. **Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei killed** in Tehran on **28 February 2026** (confirmed 1 March). An **Interim Leadership Council** ran the state.
3. **New Supreme Leader — Mojtaba Khamenei** (his son), chosen by the **Assembly of Experts** on 7 March and in office from **8 March 2026**. President **Masoud Pezeshkian** continues; Foreign Minister **Abbas Araghchi**; chief negotiator Parliament Speaker **Mohammad-Bagher Ghalibaf**.
4. **Widening** — Iran struck Israel and Gulf states; **Hezbollah** joined on **2 March**; Israel's ground operation in **south Lebanon** from 16 March; a US-brokered Israel–Lebanon truce from **16 April 2026** (extended), but Israeli strikes continue.
5. **Strait of Hormuz** — effectively closed by Iran since the war began; the **World Bank** called it "**the largest oil market disruption in history**". **Brent** rose about **65% in March 2026** (largest monthly rise ever), peaking above **US$120**; it has mostly stayed around US$90–105 since.
6. **UN Security Council** — **11 March 2026**: resolution sponsored by **Bahrain** condemning Iran's attacks on Gulf neighbours, adopted **13–0** (Russia and China abstaining); **7 April 2026**: a draft to reopen Hormuz **vetoed by Russia and China**.
7. **Casualties (approximate)** — about **21** US service members killed; Iran over **3,400** killed by August; Lebanon over **4,000**.

:::tip Why it matters for Pakistan
Pakistan's **mediation** (Islamabad Talks, Islamabad MoU) raised its diplomatic profile; the **oil shock** hit its import bill and inflation; its **Saudi defence pact** (Sept 2025) and its border with Iran made neutrality delicate; Pakistan sat on the **UN Security Council** (2025–26) during the crisis.
:::

# Chapter 6 · Gaza and Palestine

1. **UN Commission of Inquiry** (chair **Navi Pillay**) concluded on **16 September 2025** that Israel has committed **genocide** in Gaza.
2. **Recognition of Palestine** — **UK, Canada, Australia, Portugal** (21 Sept 2025); **France** (22 Sept 2025, at the UN two-state conference co-chaired by **France and Saudi Arabia**), with Belgium, Luxembourg, Malta, Monaco and Andorra. The UNGA endorsed the **New York Declaration** on the two-state solution (12 Sept 2025).
3. **Trump's 20-point plan** (29 Sept 2025) → **ceasefire 10 October 2025**; all living hostages released on 13 October; **Sharm el-Sheikh Peace Summit** (co-chaired by **Trump and el-Sisi**).
4. **UNSC Resolution 2803** (**17 November 2025**) — endorsed the plan, welcomed a **Board of Peace** and authorised an **International Stabilization Force (ISF)** until **31 December 2027**; **13–0–2** (China and Russia abstained).
5. **Board of Peace** — charter signed at **Davos, 22 January 2026**; **Pakistan** (PM Shehbaz Sharif) signed with Saudi Arabia, Türkiye, Qatar, UAE, Indonesia, Jordan, Egypt and others; **Trump chairman**; executive board includes Kushner, Rubio, Witkoff, **Tony Blair** and **Ajay Banga**.
6. **Phase Two** (January 2026) — a technocratic **National Committee for the Administration of Gaza** (chief commissioner **Ali Shaath**), disarmament and the ISF.
7. **One year on (October 2026)** — Israel controls about **70%** of Gaza; strikes continue; the Gaza Health Ministry's total is about **74,000 killed**; reconstruction has not begun; the ISF is barely deployed.
8. **ICJ — South Africa v. Israel** (Genocide Convention) — pending; the Court fixed South Africa's Reply for November 2027. ICJ advisory opinions: **climate change** (23 July 2025 — states have legal obligations) and Israel's obligations regarding UN/UNRWA aid (22 Oct 2025).
9. **ICC** — arrest warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant (Nov 2024) stand; **Prosecutor Karim Khan removed** by the Assembly of States Parties on **24 July 2026** (first removal of an ICC prosecutor); **Duterte** (arrested March 2025) — charges of crimes against humanity confirmed April 2026.
