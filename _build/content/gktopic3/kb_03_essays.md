
# Chapter 14 · Essay outlines on current issues

:::eg 1. The 27th Amendment and the independence of the judiciary
**Introduction** — independence of the judiciary as a basic feature (Arts. 2A, 175(3); *Al-Jehad Trust* PLD 1996 SC 324; *Sharaf Faridi*). **The change** — 26th Amendment (parliamentary committee appoints the CJP; constitutional benches); 27th Amendment (Federal Constitutional Court; transfer of judges through the JCP; resignations of two Supreme Court judges). **Arguments for** — specialised constitutional court (Germany, South Africa, Türkiye models); backlog in the Supreme Court; Charter of Democracy 2006 proposed a constitutional court; parliamentary supremacy. **Arguments against** — executive influence over appointments and transfers; dilution of the Supreme Court; haste and lack of consultation; immunity provisions offend equality (Art. 25). **Way forward** — transparent JCP criteria, security of tenure, consensus-based constitutional change, timely justice reform.
:::

:::eg 2. Pakistan's role as a mediator in the Iran war
Background (Twelve-Day War 2025; war from 28 Feb 2026; Hormuz); Pakistan's assets — ties with Washington (Field Marshal Munir), Tehran (neighbour), Riyadh (defence pact), Beijing; **8 April ceasefire**, **Islamabad Talks**, **Islamabad MoU**; gains — diplomatic stature, praise from the UN Secretary-General; limits — collapse in July, economic cost of the oil shock, sectarian sensitivities, Saudi commitments; lessons for a "**bridge-builder**" foreign policy (Art. 40).
:::

:::eg 3. Pakistan–Afghanistan relations
History (Durand Line, refugees, 1980s jihad, 2021 Taliban takeover); TTP sanctuaries and the surge in terrorism; Doha ceasefire (Oct 2025); the 2026 conflict (Ghazab lil Haq) and China's mediation; trade and transit (Torkham, APTTA); refugee repatriation and humanitarian law; recommendations — border management, regional mechanisms (China, Qatar, Türkiye, Russia), trade as a peace tool, engagement without recognition.
:::

:::eg 4. Pakistan–India relations after May 2025 and the Indus Waters Treaty
Pahalgam and Marka-e-Haq; nuclear deterrence and third-party (US) crisis management; India's holding the IWT in abeyance — legal analysis (no unilateral suspension; PCA awards of 2025–26); water as a national-security issue; Kashmir (Art. 370 revoked 2019; UNSC resolutions); sports and trade boycotts; way forward — legal and diplomatic pressure, water storage, internationalising the dispute, crisis-communication mechanisms.
:::

:::eg 5. The economy — from stabilisation to growth
Facts (GDP US$452 bn, growth 3.7%, per capita US$1,901, remittances US$41.6 bn, IMF EFF US$7 bn, policy rate 11.5%, debt about 69% of GDP, interest Rs 8 trillion — larger than the net federal revenue); structural problems — narrow tax base (tax-to-GDP about 10–11%), energy circular debt, loss-making SOEs, low exports, oil-import dependence (2026 shock); reforms — broadening taxes (retail, agriculture, real estate), privatisation (PIA, DISCOs), export-led growth, IT, minerals (Reko Diq), SIFC, CPEC 2.0, climate resilience.
:::

:::eg 6. Climate change and disasters
2022 and 2025 floods (Buner cloudburst; Punjab's largest flood); heatwaves; GLOFs; Art. 9A (26th Amendment) and environmental jurisprudence (*Shehla Zia* PLD 1994 SC 693; *Asghar Leghari* 2015 LHC); COP30 (Belém) and COP31 (Antalya, Nov 2026); loss and damage fund; adaptation finance; recommendations — building codes (Peshawar school collapse), early warning, reforestation, water storage, climate-resilient agriculture.
:::

:::eg 7. Governor's rule in KP — federalism under strain
Constitutional framework (Arts. 232–234, 245; past instances); the October 2026 proclamation and its grounds; parliamentary endorsement; judicial review (proclamations are justiciable — *Farooq Ahmad Khan Leghari* PLD 1999 SC 57 on emergencies; Indian *S. R. Bommai* (1994) on President's rule); impact on provincial autonomy after the 18th Amendment; recommendations — use only as a last resort, time-bound, with judicial and parliamentary oversight; political dialogue.
:::

:::eg 8. Other likely essay topics
The **UN at 80** and reform (UN80; Security Council expansion; choosing the next Secretary-General); **Gaza** — international law, ICJ and ICC; **artificial intelligence** — opportunities and regulation (EU AI Act; Pakistan's National AI Policy 2025); **regional groupings** — SCO (Pakistan's chair), BRICS, OIC; **Trump's tariffs and the world trading system**; **energy security after Hormuz**.
:::
