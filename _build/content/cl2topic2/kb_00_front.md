# How to use this knowledge base

## What Topic 2 covers

1. **Inheritance** — the general principles, the impediments, the **Hanafi** scheme of sharers, residuaries and distant kindred, exclusion, **aul** and **radd**, the **Shia** scheme, and the **MFLO s. 4** rule for orphaned grandchildren (with the 2021 Fiqh-e-Jafria provisions).
2. **Administration of the estate** — funeral expenses, debts (including dower), legacies, then shares; succession certificates (including NADRA under the 2020 Act); protection of women's inheritance.
3. **Wills (wasiyyat)** — capacity, the one-third rule, bequests to heirs, revocation; death-bed (*marz-ul-maut*) transactions.
4. **Gifts (hiba)** — declaration, acceptance and delivery of possession; *musha*; revocation; *hiba-bil-iwaz*; registration of gifts in Pakistan.
5. **Waqf** — essentials, family waqf and the 1913 Act, the mutawalli, and the **ICT Waqf Properties Act 2020**.
6. **Pre-emption (shufa)** — who may pre-empt, the three **talbs**, the law applied in Islamabad.

:::trap No bare Act for Muslim law
As in Topic 1, the bare Act is not allowed for Muhammadan Law. Learn the **shares table** (Chapter 3) by heart and practise the **worked examples** — an inheritance calculation is a favourite way of testing "practical ability".
:::
