# How to use this knowledge base

## What Topic 3 covers

1. **Definitions** and the classification of offences (s. 4; Schedule II).
2. **Criminal courts** — classes, sentencing powers (ss. 6–35), **Justices of the Peace** (ss. 22A–25).
3. **Arrest** (ss. 46–67) and **processes to compel appearance** — summons, warrant, **proclamation and attachment** (ss. 68–93).
4. **Search** (ss. 96–105, 165) and **preventive** proceedings — security, nuisance, s. 144, s. 145 (ss. 106–148).
5. **Investigation** — FIR (s. 154), statements (ss. 161–162), **confessions** (s. 164), **remand** (s. 167), report (s. 173).
6. **Cognizance**, complaints, and the **charge** (ss. 190–240).
7. **Trials** — summary, before Magistrates and before Courts of Session; s. 342; judgment; compounding (ss. 241–382B).
8. **Bail and bonds** (ss. 496–516A) and **appeals, revision, inherent powers** (ss. 404–439A, 561A).
9. **Protections for women** and the **Juvenile Justice System Act 2018**.

:::ict In the Islamabad Capital Territory
1. Magistrates are **Judicial Magistrates** (Civil Judges-cum-Judicial Magistrates) appointed under the separation of the judiciary from the executive; executive functions (ss. 107–144) are performed by **Executive Magistrates** under the ICT administration.
2. The **Sessions Judge** and, on his nomination, the **Additional Sessions Judges** are **ex-officio Justices of the Peace** (s. 25) and hear petitions under **s. 22A(6)**.
3. **Act XXXVI of 2026 (ICT only)** allows a forfeited bond to be recovered from **immovable** as well as movable property (s. 514(2)) and limits remission to **not more than half** of the surety amount (s. 514(5)).
4. **Act XXXIII of 2022** lets a person required to execute a bond furnish a **bank guarantee** instead (s. 513).
5. The **Islamabad High Court** exercises the High Court's criminal powers in the ICT.
:::
