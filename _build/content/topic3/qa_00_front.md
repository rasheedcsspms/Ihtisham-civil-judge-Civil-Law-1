# How to use these model answers

The **KP High Court 2026** paper follows the IHC syllabus most closely (CPC, Civil Courts Ordinance, Contract Act, Sale of Goods Act).

## The five parts of every answer

1. **At a glance** — the source, the marks and the exact question.
2. **The answer map** — a one-page picture; each box is one numbered heading of the answer. Draw a rough version in the exam before writing.
3. **Decoding the question** — what the examiner wants.
4. **The model answer** — headings, bare-Act quotations, one case per point, an illustration and a conclusion.
5. **Why this answer scores** — the techniques to copy.

:::tip Pakistani law wins marks in this topic
The Contract Act in force in Islamabad differs from the Indian Act in places that examiners notice. They are **ss. 30A–30C** (collateral wagering agreements void; contrast *Gherulal Parakh*), **s. 74 illustrations (d)–(g)** (penal interest and acceleration clauses), **s. 27** (goodwill exception only) and **s. 28** (not amended as in India in 1997). Quote also **Art. 17 of the Qanun-e-Shahadat** (attestation of financial documents), **Art. 127 QSO** (burden of good faith) and the **Electronic Transactions Ordinance 2002**. Each one shows that you have read the law as it applies in Pakistan.
:::
